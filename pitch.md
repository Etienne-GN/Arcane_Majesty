What if a dark fantasy saga wasn't just meant to be read, but **heard and played**?

**Arcane Majesty** is a massive creative project fusing narrative music, rich cosmic worldbuilding, and retro-modern gaming.

---

✨ **What makes Arcane Majesty unique:**

🎵 **8 Interconnected Concept Albums**  
An epic symphonic metal saga spanning centuries across a strict timeline (*The Great Darkness - 0 GD*). Every song is a narrative chapter told through the voice of **Vorgos the Stormbringer**, Universal Sentinel of the Aether.

🌌 **Deep Cosmic Structure & Lore**  
Explore the Tri-Planar Axis: **Auroria** (Celestial Realm), **Eldoria** (Material Realm), and **Nythoria** (Umbral Realm). Follow the intersecting fates of:
- **Acheron** — The First Vampire & Fallen Aurorian Seer
- **Malakar** — The Lord of Shadows & Void Disciple
- **Seraphina** — The Queen of Carnage & Leader of the Shadow Legion
- **Anya** — The Warden of the Void
- **Eldrin Nightshade** — Master Mage of Thaloria

🎮 **2D Top-Down Action RPG Prototype**  
A top-down Action RPG built with **Phaser 3 & Socket.io**, inspired by classic retro adventure games. Explore the world, battle mythical beasts, and experience the story in real-time!

🧠 **Knowledge Graph & Compendium (Neo4j)**  
Every relationship between characters, artifacts, locations, and song plots is mapped inside an interconnected graph database for seamless narrative continuity.
