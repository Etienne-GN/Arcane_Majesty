Arcane Majesty Compendium

A multi-dimensional fantasy saga across music, lore, and magical realms.

Arcane Majesty Universe

Concept: An epic fantasy multiverse chronicling the struggle between light and darkness through characters, realms, songs, and arcane powers.

Medium: Music albums, game development with Solarus, narrative fiction.

Universe: The saga includes characters like Eldrin Nightshade, Anya, Nychtoros, and Malakar across interconnected realms. The story blends mythic conflict, arcane secrets, and emotional sacrifice.

  • Character database growing (13+ key figures)

World-Building & Lore Network

Every album, track, and act tells part of a larger mythos. Key locations, magical artifacts, and alliances shape a living world in conflict. Eldoria, the Void Empire, and characters like Kael, Seraphina, and the Shapeshifter are part of a vast cosmology.

Eldoria

A once-great kingdom, now in ruins. It is the setting for Eldrin's quest to find the Heartstone of Creation.

The Empire of the Void

A realm of darkness and despair, ruled by the malevolent Nychtoros. It is where Anya's journey of transformation takes place.

Heartstone of Creation

A legendary artifact with the power to heal or destroy worlds. It is the object of Eldrin's quest and the key to saving Eldoria.

Shadow Crown of Nythoria

Malakar's crown that maintains perpetual twilight over his realm, wreathed in living shadows that whisper secrets of the void. This artifact of supreme darkness feeds on fear and despair, growing stronger with each soul trapped within Nythoria's endless night.

Arcane Majesty - Eldoria's Prophecy

  • ARPG combat with magic-based abilities and swordplay.
  • Upgradeable magic system—spells tied to Eldrin’s emotional state.
  • Exploration-heavy gameplay—hidden secrets, puzzles, and lore pieces.
  • Dynamic choices affecting relationships and the final battle.
  • Pixel-art aesthetic inspired by Zelda: Minish Cap and Crystalis.
