A Tapestry of Souls

Era II

When the primordial entity Nyktoros awakens, the universal balance is shattered. Vorgos the Stormbringer plucks a young scholar named Anya from the Celestial Plane and thrusts her into the war-torn Underworld. Discovering she is a Void-Echo, Anya must join the Nythorian resistance to seal the Void before existence is consumed by the absolute dark. A story of sacrifice, forbidden lineage, and the weight of cosmic destiny.
