The Crystal Focus

A multi-faceted prismatic lens floating in a silver casing. It allows the user to peer through the Aether and see the "Frequency of Souls." It was originally forged by Vorgos to monitor the balance between the three planes.

The Forbidden Tome

A heavy, obsidian-bound book that hums with a low-frequency vibration. Its pages are made of shadow-ink that shifts when read. It contains the "Source Code" of the Void, used by Acheron to create the first vampires and by Malakar to orchestrate the Great Darkness.

A glowing, ruby-like gem that pulses like a living heart. It is the literal anchor of the Material Plane's life-force. Stolen by Acheron during his exile, its absence caused the slow "leak" of souls that eventually destabilized the world.
