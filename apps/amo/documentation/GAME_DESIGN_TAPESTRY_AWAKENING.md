# Game Design: A Tapestry of Souls — The Awakening

**Status:** Game Design Document
**Source Material:** *A Tapestry of Souls: The Awakening* (0 GD, Era II)
**Album:** 10 songs (1 kept + 9 new)
**Platform:** Phaser 3 (Arcane Majesty engine)
**Genre:** Action RPG (main campaign) + Exploration/Combat (bonus campaign)

---

## 1. Overview

**Setting:** 0 GD — The Great Darkness. Nythoria is under siege by the Legion of Souls. The Void stirs. The world is ending.

**Two Campaigns:**

| Campaign | Protagonist | Songs | Genre | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Main: The Fall of Nythoria** | Seraphina | The Long Watch → The Unending War (7 songs) | Action RPG / Command | 7-10 hours |
| **Bonus: Torn from the Stars** | Anya | Auroria's Lament → Torn from the Stars (3 songs) | Exploration / Combat | 2-3 hours |

**Total:** 9-13 hours of gameplay

**Connection:** Both campaigns end at the same moment — Vorgos tears the Great Rift and pulls Anya from Auroria into the Underworld. This leads directly into *The Paradox of Light* (Album 2 game).

---

## 2. Main Campaign: The Fall of Nythoria

### Protagonist: Seraphina

**Class:** Warrior / Commander
**Role:** Defender of Nythoria, leader of the resistance

**Abilities:**
- **Shadow-Blade Combat:** Melee attacks with shadow-infused weapons. Fast, aggressive, close-range.
- **Commander's Aura:** Rally nearby allies, boost morale, improve defenses. Limited range, requires line of sight.
- **Tactical Planning:** Set formations, assign roles to NPC troops, plan ambushes.
- **Shadow Step:** Short-range teleport through shadows. Escape or reposition.

**Character Arc:**
1. **Defender** (Missions 1-3): Seraphina defends Nythoria's borders. She's confident, capable, in control.
2. **Commander** (Missions 4-5): The war escalates. Seraphina leads larger forces, makes hard choices.
3. **Refugee** (Mission 6): Nythoria falls. Seraphina retreats with survivors. She's broken but alive.
4. **Broken but Alive** (Mission 7): The war grinds on. Resources dwindle. Hope fades. But she persists.

### Setting: Nythoria

**The Ancestral Homeland:**
- Dark, ancient architecture — obsidian towers, shadow-infused stone
- Bioluminescent fungi and eerie violet light
- The Obsidian Citadel — seat of power
- The Shadow-Vale — training grounds
- The Black Well — ancient well of Void energy

**Map Zones:**

| Zone | Description | Missions |
|---|---|---|
| **Nythoria Outskirts** | Borderlands, first line of defense | Mission 1 |
| **Nythoria City** | Urban center, civilian populations | Mission 2 |
| **The Obsidian Walls** | Massive fortifications, siege warfare | Mission 3 |
| **The Battlefields** | Scarred terrain, abandoned camps | Mission 4 |
| **Ravenspire Ruins** | Ancestral ruins, last stand | Mission 5 |
| **Burning Nythoria** | The city falls, retreat paths | Mission 6 |
| **Scattered Camps** | Hideouts, refugee camps | Mission 7 |

### Missions

---

#### Mission 1: The Long Watch
**Song:** The Long Watch
**Setting:** Nythoria Outskirts
**Objective:** Defend against the first probes of the Legion

**Narrative:**
Seraphina patrols the border. The长夜 has lasted too long. Scouts report movement in the shadows. Something stirs in the Underworld. The长watch begins.

**Gameplay:**
- Tutorial mission — teach combat, command, abilities
- Defend against small groups of Shadow-Touched creatures
- Establish defensive positions
- First hints of the Legion's presence

**Enemies:**
- Shadow-Touched wildlife (basic)
- Void-Touched scouts (ranged)
- Corrupted spirits (fast, fragile)

**Objectives:**
1. Patrol the border
2. Defend against first attack
3. Rally troops to defensive positions
4. Hold until dawn

---

#### Mission 2: The Awakening
**Song:** The Awakening
**Setting:** Nythoria City
**Objective:** The Void stirs, citizens panic, rally defenses

**Narrative:**
The city wakes to screams. The Void is awakening. Citizens flee. The Legion's first waves strike the outer districts. Seraphina must calm the panic and organize the defense.

**Gameplay:**
- Escort civilians to safety
- Defend key buildings from Legion incursions
- Manage panic (morale system introduced)
- First appearance of Legion infantry

**Enemies:**
- Legion of Souls (infantry) — basic melee units
- Void-Touched beasts (fast, aggressive)
- Shadow Wraiths (phasing, hard to hit)

**Objectives:**
1. Evacuate civilians from the outer district
2. Defend the market square
3. Rally the city guard
4. Hold the central plaza until reinforcements arrive

---

#### Mission 3: Empire of the Void
**Song:** Empire of the Void
**Setting:** The Obsidian Walls
**Objective:** The Legion declares, massive assault begins

**Narrative:**
The Legion of Souls gathers in force. Nyktoros's herald announces the empire's declaration. The siege begins. Seraphina commands the defense of the Obsidian Walls.

**Gameplay:**
- Large-scale siege warfare
- Command multiple units (archers, infantry, mages)
- Defend against waves of attackers
- First mini-boss: Legion Commander

**Enemies:**
- Legion of Souls (infantry, archers, mages)
- Void-Touched beasts (heavier variants)
- Shadow Wraiths (more numerous)
- **Mini-boss:** Legion Commander (shadow blade, tactical AI)

**Objectives:**
1. Position troops along the walls
2. Defend against three waves of assault
3. Defeat the Legion Commander
4. Hold the walls until the Legion retreats

**Cutscene:** The Legion retreats, but Nyktoros's presence grows. The长夜 deepens.

---

#### Mission 4: March of the Legion
**Song:** March of the Legion
**Setting:** The Battlefields
**Objective:** Push back the Legion, pyrrhic victories

**Narrative:**
The war turns offensive. Seraphina leads counter-attacks to reclaim territory. Each victory costs more than the last. The Legion retreats, but the land is scarred.

**Gameplay:**
- Offensive operations — push into Legion-held territory
- Capture and hold objectives
- Resource scarcity (supplies, troops)
- Morale system — victories boost, losses drain

**Enemies:**
- Legion of Souls (entrenched positions)
- Void-Touched beasts (territorial, defensive)
- Shadow Wraiths (ambush tactics)
- **Mini-boss:** Legion War-Master (heavy, slow, devastating)

**Objectives:**
1. Capture the forward camp
2. Push through the Valley of Shadows
3. Secure the ancient bridge
4. Establish a forward base

**Cutscene:** Victory, but at cost. The troops are exhausted. The长夜 continues.

---

#### Mission 5: The First Stand
**Song:** The First Stand
**Setting:** Ravenspire Ruins
**Objective:** Seraphina and Valen make their stand

**Narrative:**
Valen is wounded. The resistance is cornered in the ruins of Ravenspire. Seraphina and Valen fight back-to-back, holding the line against overwhelming odds.

**Gameplay:**
- Co-op mechanics (Valen as AI companion)
- Defensive holdout — survive waves
- Valen provides ranged support, Seraphina melee
- Emotional narrative beats

**Enemies:**
- Legion of Souls (massive numbers)
- Void-Touched beasts (heavy variants)
- Shadow Wraiths (elite)
- **Boss:** Legion Champion (fast, aggressive, shadow abilities)

**Objectives:**
1. Defend the inner sanctum
2. Protect Valen while he recovers
3. Hold until the evacuation is complete
4. Defeat the Legion Champion

**Cutscene:** Valen is wounded but alive. The resistance must retreat. Seraphina carries Valen. The fall begins.

---

#### Mission 6: The Fall of Ravenspire
**Song:** The Fall of Ravenspire
**Setting:** Burning Nythoria
**Objective:** The city falls, retreat with survivors

**Narrative:**
Nythoria burns. The Legion floods the streets. Seraphina leads the retreat — civilians, wounded, the last remnants of the resistance. The city falls behind them.

**Gameplay:**
- Escape sequence — move through burning streets
- Escort civilians and wounded
- Avoid or fight through Legion patrols
- Moral choices: who to save, who to leave

**Enemies:**
- Legion of Souls (pursuers)
- Void-Touched beasts (rampaging)
- Shadow Wraiths (blocking paths)
- No boss — this is about survival, not conquest

**Objectives:**
1. Guide civilians through the burning district
2. Break through Legion blockades
3. Reach the evacuation point
4. Escape Nythoria before it falls

**Cutscene:** Nythoria falls. The Obsidian Citadel crumbles. Seraphina watches from the ridge. The长夜 wins. But the survivors live.

---

#### Mission 7: The Unending War
**Song:** The Unending War
**Setting:** Scattered Camps
**Objective:** War grinds on, resources dwindle, hope fades

**Narrative:**
Weeks later. The resistance hides in scattered camps. Resources are thin. Morale is low. The war continues, but there are no victories anymore — only survival.

**Gameplay:**
- Resource management — scavenge, ration, maintain
- Camp management — assign roles, train survivors
- Small-scale raids for supplies
- Emotional narrative — conversations, reflections

**Enemies:**
- Legion patrols (random encounters)
- Void-Touched wildlife (territorial)
- Shadow Wraiths (hunting parties)
- No boss — this is about endurance

**Objectives:**
1. Scavenge for supplies
2. Defend the camp from a Legion raid
3. Rescue a group of survivors
4. Hold until dawn

**Cutscene:** The长夜 continues. Seraphina looks at the sky. No dawn comes. The war is unending. But the survivors persist. The campaign ends on a note of grim determination.

### Main Campaign Boss: The Legion Champion

**Setting:** Ravenspire Ruins (Mission 5)
**Health:** High
**Damage:** High
**Speed:** Fast

**Abilities:**
- **Shadow Blade:** Fast melee combos, wide arcs
- **Shadow Step:** Teleport behind player, instant attack
- **Frenzy:** Speed and damage boost when health drops below 50%
- **Command Aura:** Buffs nearby Legion units

**Strategy:**
1. Learn attack patterns (telegraphed)
2. Use Shadow Step to reposition
3. Break the Command Aura to weaken adds
4. Exploit the frenzy window (high damage, high risk)

**Cutscene:** Valen fires the killing shot. The Champion falls. But Valen collapses — wounded. Seraphina catches him. The stand is over.

## 3. Bonus Campaign: Torn from the Stars

### Protagonist: Anya

**Class:** Scholar / Mage
**Role:** Seeker of knowledge, Void-Echo

**Abilities:**
- **Aether Sight:** See hidden truths, reveal weaknesses, detect Void energy. Core mechanic for exploration and puzzles.
- **Knowledge System:** Collect lore fragments, piece together truths about the Void. Unlocks abilities and dialogue options.
- **Void-Echo Powers:** Unlock as story progresses. Immunity to Void energy, ability to disperse shadow creatures.
- **Basic Combat:** Staff strikes, simple spells. Anya is a scholar, not a warrior. Combat is a last resort.

**Character Arc:**
1. **Curious Scholar** (Mission 8): Anya seeks knowledge in the library. She's focused, determined, but unaware of her own power.
2. **Discoverer** (Mission 9): Anya verifies her findings in the forest. She faces enemies and discovers her Void-Echo nature.
3. **Special** (Mission 10): Anya defeats the shadow creature. Vorgos sees her power. The Rift tears. She's pulled into destiny.

### Setting: Auroria

**The Celestial Plane:**
- Luminous, ethereal architecture — floating towers, crystal spires
- The Celestial Archives — vast library of floating tomes
- The Celestial Forest — bioluminescent trees, ancient spirits
- The Crystal Sanctum — seat of the Seers

**Map Zones:**

| Zone | Description | Missions |
|---|---|---|
| **Celestial Archives** | Vast library, floating shelves, luminous tomes | Mission 8 |
| **Celestial Forest** | Bioluminescent trees, ancient spirits | Mission 9 |
| **The Rift** | Tear in reality, cosmic chaos | Mission 10 |

### Missions

---

#### Mission 8: Auroria's Lament
**Song:** Auroria's Lament
**Setting:** Celestial Archives
**Objective:** Search the library for knowledge about the Void

**Narrative:**
Anya works in the Celestial Archives. The长夜 has reached even Auroria — the scholars whisper of a growing darkness. Anya searches for answers. She finds something unexpected.

**Gameplay:**
- Exploration — navigate the vast library
- Puzzle-solving — decode ancient texts, find hidden passages
- Knowledge collection — gather lore fragments about the Void
- Minimal combat — shadow creatures in the library (minor)

**Enemies:**
- Shadow Wisps (minor, easily dispersed)
- Corrupted Archives (environmental hazards — falling books, shifting shelves)

**Objectives:**
1. Navigate to the restricted section
2. Decode the ancient text about the Void
3. Find the reference to the Celestial Forest
4. Decide to verify in the forest

**Discovery:** Anya finds a text suggesting the Void can be studied, not just feared. A reference to an object in the Celestial Forest that holds Void energy.

---

#### Mission 9: The Sentinel's Gambit
**Song:** The Sentinel's Gambit
**Setting:** Celestial Forest
**Objective:** Verify findings in the forest, face shadow creatures

**Narrative:**
Anya enters the Celestial Forest. The trees glow with ancient light. The Void has touched even this place. She searches for the object mentioned in the text.

**Gameplay:**
- Exploration — navigate the forest, find the object
- Combat — face shadow creatures, Void-touched wildlife
- Puzzle-solving — use Aether Sight to reveal hidden paths
- Discovery — find the object with the shadow creature embedded

**Enemies:**
- Shadow Creatures (medium — fast, aggressive)
- Corrupted Spirits (ranged, magical)
- Void-Touched Wildlife (beasts, territorial)

**Objectives:**
1. Enter the Celestial Forest
2. Navigate to the ancient grove
3. Find the object with Void energy
4. Discover the shadow creature embedded within

**Discovery:** Anya finds a crystalline object pulsing with Void energy. As she reaches for it, a shadow creature emerges — the Shadow Keeper.

---

#### Mission 10: Torn from the Stars
**Song:** Torn from the Stars
**Setting:** Celestial Forest → The Rift → The Underworld
**Objective:** Boss fight → Vorgos cutscene → Great Rift → Anya pulled down

**Narrative:**
The Shadow Keeper attacks. Anya must fight. When she defeats it, Vorgos — watching from the Aether — sees her power. He tears the Great Rift and pulls her through.

**Gameplay:**
- Boss fight against the Shadow Keeper
- Vorgos cutscene (interactive — player watches)
- The Rift sequence (cinematic with brief combat)
- Arrival in the Underworld (setup for Album 2)

**Boss: The Shadow Keeper**

**Setting:** Celestial Forest clearing
**Health:** Medium
**Damage:** Medium
**Speed:** Medium

**Abilities:**
- **Shadow Tendrils:** Ranged attacks, tracking
- **Phase Shift:** Become intangible, reposition
- **Void Pulse:** Area-of-effect damage
- **Enrage:** Speed boost when low health

**Strategy:**
1. Use Aether Sight to reveal weaknesses during Phase Shift
2. Dodge Shadow Tendrils (telegraphed)
3. Attack during vulnerability windows
4. Disperse with Void-Echo power when available

**Cutscene (Interactive):**
- Anya defeats the Shadow Keeper
- The forest goes silent
- Vorgos appears in the sky — a storm of grey
- He watches Nythoria (cross-cut to Seraphina's campaign)
- He sees Anya defeat the shadow creature
- He realizes she's special — a Void-Echo
- He makes his choice

**The Rift Sequence:**
- The sky tears open — the Great Rift
- Anya is pulled toward it
- She resists, but the pull is irresistible
- She falls through the Rift — cosmic chaos, Void energy
- She lands in the Underworld

**Ending:**
- Anya wakes in darkness
- She hears a voice: "Who are you?"
- It's Valen — wounded, hiding
- The Underworld stretches before them
- **The Paradox of Light begins**

## 6. Development Notes

### Priority Order
1. **Main Campaign** (Seraphina) — core game, 7 missions
2. **Bonus Campaign** (Anya) — unlockable after main campaign, 3 missions
3. **Polish** — cutscenes, voice acting, music integration

### Album Integration
- Each mission is tied to a song
- Music plays during key moments
- Lyrics appear in cutscenes (subtle, not explicit)
- The album is the narrative backbone

### Future Games
- **Game 2:** *The Paradox of Light* (Anya's story, Album 2)
- **Game 3:** *Dawn of Ravenspire* (Anya's story, Album 3)
- **Game 4:** *Eldoria's Prophecy* (Eldrin's story, already designed)
