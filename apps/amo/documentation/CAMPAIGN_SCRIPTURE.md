# AC1: Eldoria's Prophecy - Campaign Scripture (Strict Lore Edition)

**Status:** Director's Master Script for Claude (Coder)
**Source Material:** *Eldoria's Prophecy* (Era III Mid, ~500 AGD) — Album + Novel
**Mandate:** Strict adherence to Album Plot, Lyrics, and Book canon. The album wins on any conflict.

---

## Act I: The Call of the Architect

### Track 1: Echoes of Stone (The Tower)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin discovers the "Gap" in history and geological anomalies.
*   **Quest:** "The Unreadable Record"
    *   **Objective:** Search the Archives for mentions of 0 GD.
    *   **Lore Beat:** The "Echoes" in the stone suggest the world has catastrophically ended before. The records of 0 GD were tidied, not missing. His career's thesis — that the record is a lie — is confirmed.
    *   **Gameplay:** Investigation of the Tower's basement strata reveals the Echoes (spectral ripples in the ground). Scholar's Eye echo zones show ghostly reconstructions of what the strata once held.

### Track 2: Dreamweaver's Call (The Vision)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Vorgos (The Stormbringer) pulls Eldrin into a vision of 900 AGD.
*   **Quest:** "Summons from the Deep"
    *   **Objective:** Survive the vision (wave survival in a distorted reality).
    *   **Lore Beat:** Eldrin sees the "Violet Sky" — a Star-Guard shattering, Anya weeping over Kael. Vorgos speaks: "The Lock will fail. Find the Anchor." It is not a request; it is a command from the Aether.
    *   **Ending:** Eldrin wakes with the Compass of the Storm burned into his mind — coordinates east, a deadline before the century turns. The Compass becomes the primary quest tracker (HUD element).

### Track 3: Odyssey's Dawn (The Journey Begins)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin seals his tower and walks east into a world that doesn't know it's in danger.
*   **Quest:** "The Solitary Way"
    *   **Objective:** Exit the Tower, travel through the East Road villages.
    *   **Lore Beat:** "The scholar's life is mine to live no more... They do not know the reason why I fly." NPCs speak of simple things, oblivious to the cosmic threat.
    *   **Event:** On the East Road, the first shadows of the hunt surface — Legion agents asking quiet questions about "the Anchor." A learned traveler named **Oren** joins Eldrin, offering knowledge and company.
    *   **Companion System:** Oren joins as a party member. He carries a pack, splits firewood, reads the snow. His dialogue is warm and scholarly. The player should trust him.

## Act II: Trials and Tribulations

### Track 4: Summit of Despair (The Ascent)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Climbing the treacherous mountain pass; the onsla of doubt.
*   **Quest:** "The Icy Crown"
    *   **Objective:** Reach the peak of the mountain during a mana-storm.
    *   **Lore Beat:** On the pass, Eldrin finds the frozen camp of fourteen dead — the work of the Legion's Whisperer. The cold is not just physical; the Aether runs thin.
    *   **Boss:** **Malphas, the Whisperer of Doubt**. He feeds on fear: illusions of allies and past failures crowd the whiteout. Malphas does not fight directly — he whispers, he shows windows into doubt. The player must use Aether Sight to break the anchor-thread. Malphas retreats, vowing to find Eldrin "where the road is loneliest."
    *   **Mechanic:** The Whisperer's attacks are psychological — screen distortion, false enemy spawns, reversed controls. Aether Sight reveals the truth.

### Track 5: Sylvan Sanctuary (The Hidden Forest)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Finding an impossible green valley amidst the snow.
*   **Quest:** "The Sacred Trust"
    *   **Objective:** Pass the trial of the Elemental guarding the valley.
    *   **Lore Beat:** The Elemental trials Eldrin on intent: "Why do you seek the light of day?" Eldrin answers honestly — he seeks the Stone to save the Lock.
    *   **NPC: The Hermit.** A failed former Keeper, his spirit bound to the forest Elemental. He grants Eldrin formal mastery of Aether Sight. He warns: the way ahead holds a fire that cannot be passed, and a guardian that cannot be commanded or bargained with.
    *   **Key Dialogue:** The Hermit reads Oren with cold, weighing stillness — but says nothing. This is a missed warning the player may catch on replay.
    *   **Atmosphere:** The only peace on the road. The price of it is the fire ahead.

### Track 6: Treachery's Bite (The Betrayal)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** At the first landing of the Fire Gate, Oren's mask comes off.
*   **Quest:** "The Sudden Sting"
    *   **Objective:** Descend into the Fire Gate with Oren.
    *   **Lore Beat:** The gate's black mouth swallows them. The runes grow louder in the dark. The heat closes over them like a hand.
    *   **Event (NOT a boss fight):** At the first landing, Oren strikes. The black knife — not the honest blade he carried all winter, but a blade made of darkness. Eldrin goes down. Oren reveals: he is Voraun's agent. He has been Voraun's for thirty years. His scholarship, his kindness, his friendship — all real, all part of the work. "The friendship was a job. But it was the best job I ever drew."
    *   **What Oren takes:** The journal with the bearings and coordinates. He does NOT take the Compass (the needle in Eldrin's skull — it cannot be given or taken).
    *   **What Oren leaves:** Eldrin's rune-kit, the food, the broken staff. Professional courtesy or the last of the kindness.
    *   **Oren descends ahead**, carrying the record of the world into the labyrinth.
    *   **Aftermath:** Eldrin lies on the black glass at the first landing, bleeding. He binds the wound with the rune-kit. He reads the ledger of the winter and strikes it out. He does NOT climb out of the Fire Gate. He stays in the descent.
    *   **Design Note:** This is a narrative cutscene, not combat. The betrayal should feel like a door closing. The player loses Oren as a companion permanently.

### Track 7: The Solitary Path (Isolation)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Bleeding and alone in the descent, Eldrin embraces solitude.
*   **Quest:** "Until the Bitter End"
    *   **Objective:** Walk alone deeper into the Fire Gate's labyrinth.
    *   **Lore Beat:** "The solitary path, I walk alone... No friends to lose, no trust to break." Eldrin reaches for Vorgos and finds silence. The storm does not answer. He accepts that he is a pawn on a cosmic board — and that the pawn moves anyway.
    *   **The Vow:** Eldrin speaks aloud: "I walk alone. I have no friends to lose and no trust to break. I have a needle in my skull and a wound in my side and a stone at the end of a labyrinth of fire, and I am the only one walking to it."
    *   **Atmosphere:** No companion. No music. Only the runes humming, the heat, the needle pointing east. The player feels the weight of the solitude.
    *   **Mechanic:** The Compass of the Storm is the only guide. No minimap markers. The player must navigate by the needle's direction and the rune patterns on the walls.

### Track 8: Inferno's Trial (The Crucible)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Navigating the Inferno Labyrinth, territory of Xarathos the Pyre-Lord.
*   **Quest:** "The Burning Gate"
    *   **Objective:** Cross the labyrinth of fire, overcome its guardians, reach the heart of the blaze.
    *   **Lore Beat:** A gate of fire calls Eldrin by his given name. The Legion has seeded the labyrinth with his identity — rune-puzzles tuned to his magic, golems bearing his sigil, Magma-Eaters that track his Aether signature.
    *   **Enemies:** Obsidian golems (rune-puzzles), Magma-Eaters (Aether-draining), Cinder-Souls (fast, aggressive).
    *   **Boss:** **Xarathos, the Pyre-Lord** — a Fallen Aurorian, one of the three Void Generals. "Was made of light and spent so long being hungry the light went wrong in him."
    *   **Boss Mechanic:** Xarathos unleashes a **Super-Nova** — a mana-stealing drain that pulls the player's Aether. The counter: the rune-drain. Eldrin turns the drain back, using the labyrinth's own hunger against the Pyre-Lord. The Thirst is Xarathos's flaw.
    *   **Xarathos's Dying Words:** "Voraun will have you at the door. He has been waiting the longest." This is a warning, not a threat — Xarathos knows he was a tool, and the real danger is the one who waits.
    *   **Exit:** Eldrin emerges from the mountain's far side. The ruins of the golden age rise ahead.

## Act III: The Heartstone & The Eternal Guard

### Track 9: Eldoria's Heartbeat (The Discovery)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Entering the Ruins of Eldoria — a graveyard of the golden age.
*   **Quest:** "The Cosmic Seed"
    *   **Objective:** Penetrate the ruins, reach the Heartstone of Creation, survive Voraun's ambush.
    *   **Lore Beat:** The Ruins of Eldoria are the last trace of the ancient realm, fallen before the Great Darkness. The approach is a rune-dead gate sealed by Voraun's silence.
    *   **Aetheric Witness:** Eldrin sees the golden age overlaid on the ruins — ghostly spires, phantom citizens, the pulse of the Heartstone echoing through the floor like a heartbeat.
    *   **Voraun:** The Silent Executioner has been sitting at the door of the keeping for months, waiting. He is not here to seize the Stone — he is here to prevent its use. His runic reflection mirrors magic back; his shadow-blade reaches across the ward's line.
    *   **The Stone Accepts:** Eldrin reaches the Heartstone. The resonance syncs to him — it is a plain stone, not a jewel, not a fire, but a pulse. When Voraun reaches for it, the ward burns his hand. The unworthy hand is rejected.
    *   **Voraun's Death:** Voraun dies at the ward. His dying truth: he was sent to PREVENT the Stone's use, not to seize it. "I was never here for the prize, keeper. I was here so that it would never be used." The dark behind the Legion wants the Lock to fail. The stone is the cure, and the dark wants the cure destroyed.
    *   **The Ancient Door:** Beyond the chamber, the door appears — older than the valley, older than the keeping, with unreadable runes.
    *   **Design Note:** The Voraun encounter is not a traditional boss fight. It is a narrative confrontation — Voraun reaches for the Stone and is burned by the ward. The player does not fight him; the Stone defends itself.

### Track 10: Heart of War (The Final Stand)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** The Shadow Balrog rises from the floor before the Ancient Door.
*   **Quest:** "The Battle's Cry"
    *   **Objective:** Face the Shadow Balrog — not to defeat it, but to pass it.
    *   **Lore Beat:** "A shadow rises from the floor, the guardian of the ancient door. It hungers for the light I hold." The Balrog is ancient, untamable, subservient to no one. It has guarded the door since before the world's first ending.
    *   **Boss: The Shadow Balrog.** This is NOT a fight the player can win by force. The Balrog eats runes, absorbs spells, and its hunger pins the player down. Every attack is absorbed. Every spell is consumed.
    *   **Boss Mechanic:** The Balrog has three phases:
        1. **Aggression:** Eldrin fights as a mage — spells, runes, Aether Sight. The Balrog takes everything. "It was not an enemy. It was not guarding the door the way Voraun had guarded the gate, with a purpose and a prophecy and a blade."
        2. **Exhaustion:** Eldrin's spells are consumed. The staff is broken. The sight shows nothing but hunger. Force has failed utterly.
        3. **Yielding:** Eldrin stops demanding. He stands as what the Stone is meant for — its intended guardian. The Balrog settles. Not defeated, not commanded, not tamed. It yields to the one the Stone accepts. "The shadow that had guarded the ancient door for longer than the world's first ending had not been conquered. It simply settled, the way a tide settles, when the one it had been keeping for stopped trying to force it."
    *   **The Door Opens.** The passage beyond stands open, and it had been open all along.

### Track 11: The Weight of Eternity (The Fate)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Vorgos reveals the true price.
*   **Quest:** "The Heavy Crown"
    *   **Objective:** Accept the 400-year vigil.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Vorgos steps from time — a storm of grey. He offers the test: "Put down the Stone, and walk away." Eldrin refuses.
    *   **The Condition:** Eldrin must guard the Stone for 400 years, until a hero not yet born needs the key in 900 AGD. His friends will die. His world will change. He will wait behind an iron door while the centuries fold.
    *   **The Three Monuments:** Vorgos explains — Kael as the door, Anya as the warden, Eldrin as the stone-keeper. Three monuments of the age. Three who hold what cannot be held alone.
    *   **Eldrin's Choice:** "Four hundred years. A hundred years, and then three more. I will hold it." The weight of a life paused for a future he will never see.
    *   **Design Note:** This is a dialogue scene, not gameplay. The player chooses to accept the vigil. There is no alternative ending.

### Track 12: Dawn's Embrace (The Epilogue)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin returns to Thaloria and begins the watch.
*   **Quest:** "The Silent Vow"
    *   **Objective:** Return to Thaloria, re-seal the sanctum, begin the vigil.
    *   **Lore Beat:** "I lock the sanctum, leave the gate, and step into my written fate." Eldrin seals the chamber that holds the Heartstone. The ward of the keeping goes up — the same ward that burned Voraun.
    *   **The Blind Prophet:** The world calls him the "Blind Prophet" — metaphor only, for his sight has never failed. He holds a cure that does nothing yet, for a future he will never see.
    *   **Vorgos's Dream Visit:** In a dream, Vorgos visits: "You will not see it, but it will come." The Star-Guard is not ready. The hero is not yet born. The vigil continues.
    *   **Final Image:** Eldrin sitting in the tower, the stone at his side, the pulse steady in his chest. The seasons change. The years fold. The watcher holds the held breath. The vigil is the whole of the world's defense.
    *   **Sequel Seed:** The darkness behind the Legion is still waiting. The door it built is still coming down. The prophecy is still owed. But that is another story.
    *   **Design Note:** Epilogue is a montage — time-lapse of seasons changing while Eldrin remains motionless. No gameplay. The camera holds on the tower as the years pass.

## Director's Notes

### Key Lore Rules
1. **Music is the primary medium.** Songs/albums are the in-world telling of the story. Characters NEVER reference songs or albums.
2. **The album wins on any conflict.** If the book and album disagree, the album version is used for the game.
3. **Oren's betrayal is NOT a boss fight.** It is a narrative event. The player loses a companion, not a combat encounter.
4. **The Shadow Balrog cannot be defeated by force.** The player must yield, not fight. This is a mechanics-as-narrative moment.
5. **Voraun dies by the ward, not by Eldrin's hand.** The Stone defends itself.
6. **The "Blind Prophet" name is metaphor only.** Eldrin's sight has never failed.
7. **New game content must set `origin: "game"` in the Neo4j DB.**
