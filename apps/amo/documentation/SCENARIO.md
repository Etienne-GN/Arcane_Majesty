# AC1: Eldoria's Prophecy - Game Scenario & Lore Directive

**Status:** Lore-Master Directive for Claude (Coder)
**Source Material:** *Eldoria's Prophecy* (Era III Mid, ~500 AGD) — Album + Novel
**Protagonist:** Eldrin Nightshade (The Blind Prophet)

## 1. Narrative Core (The "Lore Anchor")

This game is **not** a generic fantasy quest. It is a **Cosmic Contingency Operation** orchestrated by **Vorgos the Stormbringer**.

*   **The Conflict:** At 0 GD, Anya sealed Nyktoros in the Underworld, becoming the Eternal Warden. The Void has been leaking since. Around 600 AGD, Kael reclaimed the Aurorian Spark and became the "Living Lock" — the only barrier between the world and total annihilation. The resonance is slowly killing Kael. If he fails, the universe ends in ~900 AGD.
*   **The Mission:** Eldrin is activated in 500 AGD to find the **Heartstone of Creation**. This artifact is the keystone of the keeping — a stone that synchronizes with its guardian and holds the resonance of the Lock stable. Eldrin's job is to secure it so it can be used 400 years later to stabilize the Lock.
*   **The Tragedy:** Eldrin must accept that he will never see the world saved. He is the bridge. He must become a solitary guardian, waiting in silence for four centuries behind an iron door while the years fold.

## 2. Gameplay & Scenario Mapping

### Prologue: The Tower of Archives (Echoes of Stone)
*   **Goal:** Tutorial & World Building.
*   **Atmosphere:** Dusty, quiet, scholarly.
*   **Event:** Eldrin finds the "Gap" in the archives — records of 0 GD that were tidied, not missing. Interaction with books/scrolls triggers the first vision of the Echoes.
*   **Lore Note:** The "Echoes" are literal. Use sound design or visual ripples to show that Eldrin senses the world "breaking" before anyone else. His career's thesis — that the record is a lie — is confirmed.

### Level 1: The Vision Rift (Dreamweaver's Call)
*   **Goal:** Introduction to Combat & Magic.
*   **Setting:** A surreal, distorted version of the world (900 AGD).
*   **Event:** Vorgos (The Stormbringer) appears as a Giant Eye/Storm. He shows Eldrin the "Violet Sky" — a Star-Guard shattering, Anya weeping over Kael. Vorgos speaks: "The Lock will fail. Find the Anchor." It is not a request; it is a command from the Aether.
*   **Mechanic:** Eldrin receives the **Compass of the Storm** — not a physical item, but a needle burned into his mind. An unremovable awareness of direction and deadline. It becomes the primary quest tracker (HUD element: a faint blue needle pointing east).

### Level 2: The East Road & The Companion (Odyssey's Dawn)
*   **Goal:** Exploration & Companion Introduction.
*   **Setting:** East Road villages, neutral settlements.
*   **Event:** Eldrin seals his tower and walks east into a world that doesn't know it's in danger. NPCs speak of simple things, oblivious to the cosmic threat.
*   **Companion: Oren.** On the East Road, a learned traveler named Oren joins Eldrin. He carries a pack, splits firewood, reads the snow. His dialogue is warm and scholarly. He offers knowledge and company. **The player should trust him.**
*   **Lore Beat:** "The scholar's life is mine to live no more... They do not know the reason why I fly." The first shadows of the hunt surface — Legion agents asking quiet questions about "the Anchor."

### Level 3: The Summit of Despair
*   **Goal:** Exploration & Survival. Introduction to the Legion's threat.
*   **Setting:** Snowy mountain passes during a mana-storm.
*   **Enemy Type:** Corrupted wildlife, "Shadow-touched" remnants.
*   **Event:** On the pass, Eldrin finds the frozen camp of fourteen dead — the work of the Legion's Whisperer. The cold is not just physical; the Aether runs thin.
*   **Boss: Malphas, the Whisperer of Doubt.** He feeds on fear: illusions of allies and past failures crowd the whiteout. Malphas does not fight directly — he whispers, he shows windows into doubt. The player must use Aether Sight to break the anchor-thread. Malphas retreats, vowing to find Eldrin "where the road is loneliest."
*   **Mechanic:** The Whisperer's attacks are psychological — screen distortion, false enemy spawns, reversed controls. Aether Sight reveals the truth.

### Level 4: The Sylvan Sanctuary
*   **Goal:** Skill Unlocks & Lore Deep-Dive.
*   **Setting:** An impossible green valley amidst the snow — a place the snow does not touch.
*   **Event:** The Elemental trials Eldrin on intent: "Why do you seek the light of day?" Eldrin answers honestly — he seeks the Stone to save the Lock.
*   **Key NPC: The Hermit.** A failed former Keeper, his spirit bound to the forest Elemental. He grants Eldrin formal mastery of **Aether Sight** (allows seeing hidden paths, rune-wards, and the true nature of enemies).
*   **Warning:** The Hermit warns: the way ahead holds a fire that cannot be passed, and a guardian that cannot be commanded or bargained with. He reads Oren with cold, weighing stillness — but says nothing. This is a missed warning the player may catch on replay.
*   **Atmosphere:** The only peace on the road. The price of it is the fire ahead.

### Level 5: The Fire Gate — Betrayal (Treachery's Bite)
*   **Goal:** Narrative Twist. The player loses their companion.
*   **Setting:** The first landing of the Fire Gate — a black mouth in the mountainside, runes humming in the dark.
*   **Event (NOT a boss fight):** At the first landing, Oren's mask comes off. The black knife — not the honest blade he carried all winter, but a blade made of darkness. Eldrin goes down.
*   **Oren's Revelation:** He is Voraun's agent. He has been Voraun's for thirty years. His scholarship, his kindness, his friendship — all real, all part of the work. "The friendship was a job. But it was the best job I ever drew."
*   **What Oren takes:** The journal with the bearings and coordinates. He does NOT take the Compass (the needle in Eldrin's skull — it cannot be given or taken).
*   **What Oren leaves:** Eldrin's rune-kit, the food, the broken staff. Professional courtesy or the last of the kindness.
*   **Aftermath:** Oren descends ahead, carrying the record of the world into the labyrinth. Eldrin lies on the black glass at the first landing, bleeding. He binds the wound with the rune-kit. He does NOT climb out of the Fire Gate. He stays in the descent.
*   **Design Note:** This is a narrative cutscene, not combat. The betrayal should feel like a door closing. The player loses Oren as a companion permanently.

### Level 6: The Solitary Path
*   **Goal:** Isolation & Acceptance.
*   **Setting:** The descent into the Fire Gate's labyrinth, alone.
*   **Event:** Eldrin reaches for Vorgos and finds silence. The storm does not answer. He accepts that he is a pawn on a cosmic board — and that the pawn moves anyway.
*   **The Vow:** Eldrin speaks aloud: "I walk alone. I have no friends to lose and no trust to break. I have a needle in my skull and a wound in my side and a stone at the end of a labyrinth of fire, and I am the only one walking to it."
*   **Atmosphere:** No companion. No music. Only the runes humming, the heat, the needle pointing east. The player feels the weight of the solitude.
*   **Mechanic:** The Compass of the Storm is the only guide. No minimap markers. The player must navigate by the needle's direction and the rune patterns on the walls.

### Level 7: The Inferno Labyrinth (Inferno's Trial)
*   **Goal:** Dungeon Crawling / Puzzle Solving / Boss Fight.
*   **Setting:** The heart of the Fire Gate — magma rivers, obsidian corridors, rune-puzzles.
*   **Event:** A gate of fire calls Eldrin by his given name. The Legion has seeded the labyrinth with his identity — rune-puzzles tuned to his magic, golems bearing his sigil, Magma-Eaters that track his Aether signature.
*   **Enemies:** Obsidian golems (rune-puzzles), Magma-Eaters (Aether-draining), Cinder-Souls (fast, aggressive).
*   **Boss: Xarathos, the Pyre-Lord.** A Fallen Aurorian, one of the three Void Generals. "Was made of light and spent so long being hungry the light went wrong in him."
*   **Boss Mechanic:** Xarathos unleashes a **Super-Nova** — a mana-stealing drain that pulls the player's Aether. The counter: the **rune-drain**. Eldrin turns the drain back, using the labyrinth's own hunger against the Pyre-Lord. The Thirst is Xarathos's flaw.
*   **Xarathos's Dying Words:** "Voraun will have you at the door. He has been waiting the longest." This is a warning, not a threat — Xarathos knows he was a tool, and the real danger is the one who waits.
*   **Exit:** Eldrin emerges from the mountain's far side. The ruins of the golden age rise ahead.

### Level 8: The Ruins of Eldoria (Eldoria's Heartbeat)
*   **Goal:** The Search for the Artifact. Narrative confrontation with Voraun.
*   **Setting:** A graveyard of the golden age — the last trace of the ancient realm, fallen before the Great Darkness.
*   **Event:** The approach is a rune-dead gate sealed by Voraun's silence. Eldrin sees the golden age overlaid on the ruins — ghostly spires, phantom citizens, the pulse of the Heartstone echoing through the floor like a heartbeat.
*   **Voraun:** The Silent Executioner has been sitting at the door of the keeping for months, waiting. He is not here to seize the Stone — he is here to prevent its use.
*   **The Stone Accepts:** Eldrin reaches the Heartstone. The resonance syncs to him — it is a plain stone, not a jewel, not a fire, but a pulse. When Voraun reaches for it, the ward burns his hand. The unworthy hand is rejected.
*   **Voraun's Death:** Voraun dies at the ward. His dying truth: he was sent to PREVENT the Stone's use, not to seize it. "I was never here for the prize, keeper. I was here so that it would never be used." The dark behind the Legion wants the Lock to fail. The stone is the cure, and the dark wants the cure destroyed.
*   **The Ancient Door:** Beyond the chamber, the door appears — older than the valley, older than the keeping, with unreadable runes.
*   **Design Note:** The Voraun encounter is not a traditional boss fight. It is a narrative confrontation — Voraun reaches for the Stone and is burned by the ward. The player does not fight him; the Stone defends itself.

### Level 9: The Shadow Balrog (Heart of War)
*   **Goal:** The Final Stand. Not a fight — a yielding.
*   **Setting:** Before the Ancient Door in the Ruins of Eldoria.
*   **Event:** The Shadow Balrog rises from the floor — the guardian of the ancient door. "A shadow rises from the floor, the guardian of the ancient door. It hungers for the light I hold." It is ancient, untamable, subservient to no one.
*   **Boss: The Shadow Balrog.** This is NOT a fight the player can win by force. The Balrog eats runes, absorbs spells, and its hunger pins the player down. Every attack is absorbed. Every spell is consumed.
*   **Boss Mechanic (Three Phases):**
    1. **Aggression:** Eldrin fights as a mage — spells, runes, Aether Sight. The Balrog takes everything. "It was not an enemy. It was not guarding the door the way Voraun had guarded the gate, with a purpose and a prophecy and a blade."
    2. **Exhaustion:** Eldrin's spells are consumed. The staff is broken. The sight shows nothing but hunger. Force has failed utterly.
    3. **Yielding:** Eldrin stops demanding. He stands as what the Stone is meant for — its intended guardian. The Balrog settles. Not defeated, not commanded, not tamed. It yields to the one the Stone accepts. "The shadow that had guarded the ancient door for longer than the world's first ending had not been conquered. It simply settled, the way a tide settles, when the one it had been keeping for stopped trying to force it."
*   **The Door Opens.** The passage beyond stands open, and it had been open all along.

### Level 10: The Weight of Eternity
*   **Goal:** The True Price. Dialogue scene, not gameplay.
*   **Setting:** The passage beyond the Ancient Door. Vorgos steps from time.
*   **Event:** Vorgos offers the test: "Put down the Stone, and walk away." Eldrin refuses.
*   **The Condition:** Eldrin must guard the Stone for 400 years, until a hero not yet born needs the key in 900 AGD. His friends will die. His world will change. He will wait behind an iron door while the centuries fold.
*   **The Three Monuments:** Vorgos explains — Kael as the door, Anya as the warden, Eldrin as the stone-keeper. Three monuments of the age. Three who hold what cannot be held alone.
*   **Eldrin's Choice:** "Four hundred years. A hundred years, and then three more. I will hold it."
*   **Design Note:** The player chooses to accept the vigil. There is no alternative ending.

### Epilogue: Dawn's Embrace
*   **Goal:** Narrative Resolution. Time-lapse montage.
*   **Event:** Eldrin returns to Thaloria, re-seals the sanctum, begins the watch. The ward of the keeping goes up — the same ward that burned Voraun.
*   **The Blind Prophet:** The world calls him the "Blind Prophet" — metaphor only, for his sight has never failed. He holds a cure that does nothing yet, for a future he will never see.
*   **Vorgos's Dream Visit:** In a dream, Vorgos visits: "You will not see it, but it will come." The Star-Guard is not ready. The hero is not yet born. The vigil continues.
*   **Sequel Seed:** The darkness behind the Legion is still waiting. The door it built is still coming down. The prophecy is still owed. But that is another story.
*   **Visual:** Eldrin sitting in the tower, the stone at his side, the pulse steady in his chest. The seasons change. The years fold. The watcher holds the held breath. The vigil is the whole of the world's defense.
*   **Design Note:** No gameplay. The camera holds on the tower as the years pass.

## 3. Technical Directives for Claude

1.  **Vorgos's Presence:** The Stormbringer should be a recurring visual/audio motif (thunder, violet flashes). He appears in the vision (Level 1), in the dream (Epilogue), and steps from time (Level 10).
2.  **The Compass:** The `Compass of the Storm` is a mental needle — not a physical item. It cannot be given, taken, or dropped. It appears as a faint blue needle on the HUD pointing toward the Heartstone.
3.  **Magic Style:** Eldrin's magic is "Scholar-Magic" — runic, geometric, and blue/violet in color (Aether-based). Refer to the full documentation suite (`magic_system.md`, `combat_system.md`, `equipment_tiers.md`, `alchemy_and_healing.md`, `economy_and_inventory.md`, `investigation_and_dialogue.md`, `audio_design.md`) for the complete RPG framework.
4.  **World State:** The world in 500 AGD is "The Silent Century." It's peaceful but "wrong." NPCs should be oblivious to the cosmic threat. Magic use here is a "Lighthouse" that attracts predators. Refer to `data/lore/bestiary.md` for enemy types and the "Ping" system.
5.  **The Endings:** We are sticking to the **True Ending (Restored Balance/Eternal Guard)**. Eldrin accepts the burden. There are no alternative endings.
6.  **Oren's Betrayal:** This is a narrative event, NOT a boss fight. The player loses a companion, not a combat encounter. The betrayal happens at the first landing of the Fire Gate.
7.  **The Shadow Balrog:** Cannot be defeated by force. The player must yield, not fight. This is a mechanics-as-narrative moment — three phases of escalating futility followed by humble acceptance.
8.  **Media-Model Rule:** Characters NEVER reference songs or albums in-world. The album songs are external retellings; the novel is Eldrin's in-world story. In the game, replace any "the songs tell" phrasing with "the old tales" / "the vision" / "the record."
9.  **Origin Rule:** New game-only content (locations, NPCs, events) must set `origin: "game"` in the Neo4j DB. Album-derived content uses `origin: "album"`.
10. **"Blind Prophet":** This name is metaphor only. Eldrin's sight has never failed. Never treat it as literal blindness.
