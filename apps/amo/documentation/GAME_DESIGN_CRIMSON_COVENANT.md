# Game Design: Crimson Covenant

**Status:** Game Design Document
**Source Material:** *Crimson Covenant* (~500 to 100 BGD, Era I)
**Album:** 13 songs
**Platform:** Phaser 3 (Arcane Majesty engine)
**Genre:** Stealth / Action RPG / Exploration
**Protagonist:** Acheron (The First Vampire)

---

## 1. Overview

**Setting:** Era I — The ancient world before the Great Darkness. Eldoria is young. Humanity is rising. The Void is dormant but present.

**Story:** Acheron, exiled Seer of Auroria, arrives in Eldoria with the Forbidden Tome. He creates the first vampires, builds a hidden empire, wages shadow wars, and ultimately realizes the Tome is too dangerous — he discards it into the Black Well in Nythoria, directly setting up *Vows of Silence* where Malakar finds it.

**Tone:** Dark, predatory, tragic. Acheron is not a hero — he is a tyrant, a visionary, and ultimately a prisoner of his own creation. The player experiences the rise and fall of a vampire empire through the eyes of its creator.

**Gameplay Mix:**

| Phase | Missions | Gameplay | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rise** (Songs 1-4) | 4 | Stealth, combat, infiltration | Hunt, flee, build the Covenant |
| **Empire** (Songs 5-7) | 3 | Strategy, underground exploration | War, rule, build the Heart-Citadel |
| **Fall** (Songs 8-13) | 6 | Combat, stealth, tragedy | Resistance, disband, curse, hiding the book |

**Total:** 13 missions, 10-14 hours of gameplay

**Sequel:** *Vows of Silence* — Malakar finds the Tome, rises to power, and destroys Acheron's physical body (The Threshold Assassination).

## 2. Protagonist: Acheron

**Class:** Seer / Vampire Lord
**Role:** Founder of the Crimson Covenant, The First Vampire

**Abilities:**

### Phase 1: Seer (Missions 1-2)
- **Aether Sight:** See hidden truths, reveal weaknesses, detect Void energy
- **Planar Frequency:** Cross between planes, sense disturbances
- **Basic Combat:** Staff strikes, simple spells

### Phase 2: Vampire Lord (Missions 3-7)
- **Blood Drain:** Steal life force from enemies, heal self
- **Shadow Step:** Teleport through shadows
- **Command:** Rally vampire allies, set formations
- **The Thirst:** Powerful but dangerous — lose control if overused

### Phase 3: The Exile Again (Missions 11-13)
- **Void Sense:** Track the Tome, sense disturbances in the Void
- **Shadow Cloak:** Blend into shadows, avoid detection
- **Last Stand:** Desperate abilities, high risk, high reward

**Character Arc:**
1. **Exile** (Missions 1-2): Acheron arrives in Eldoria, fleeing Auroria. He's desperate, brilliant, dangerous.
2. **Creator** (Missions 3-4): He creates the first vampires, builds the Covenant. He's a visionary, a tyrant.
3. **Emperor** (Missions 5-7): He rules the hidden empire. He's powerful, paranoid, losing control.
4. **The Reckoning** (Missions 8-10): The resistance rises. Nyktoros stirs. He realizes the empire is doomed.
5. **The Exile Again** (Missions 11-13): He disbands the Covenant, reflects on the curse, and hides the Tome — becoming what he was when he arrived: alone, dangerous, and running.

## 4. Missions

---

### Phase 1: The Rise (Songs 1-4)

---

#### Mission 1: Ancient Blood
**Song:** Ancient Blood
**Setting:** Eldorian Coast
**Objective:** Arrive in Eldoria, discover the Forbidden Tome, create the first vampires

**Narrative:**
Acheron is banished from Auroria. He crosses the Aether and arrives in Eldoria — a world he does not know. He finds the Forbidden Tome in the wreckage. Its power whispers to him. He begins rewriting bloodlines.

**Gameplay:**
- Tutorial — teach movement, Aether Sight, basic combat
- Explore the coast, find the shipwreck
- Discover the Forbidden Tome
- First transformation — Acheron becomes the first vampire
- Hunt animals to feed (introduce Blood Drain)

**Enemies:**
- Wildlife (deer, wolves) — prey
- No human enemies yet

**Cutscene:** Acheron reads the Tome. His body changes. The Thirst awakens. He is the first.

---

#### Mission 2: Primal Pact
**Song:** Primal Pact
**Setting:** Eldorian Villages
**Objective:** Unify the vampire clans under a sacred oath

**Narrative:**
Acheron discovers other vampires — scattered, feral, hiding. He must unite them or destroy them. He finds the first clans in the hidden vaults beneath Eldorian cities.

**Gameplay:**
- Stealth — avoid human authorities while moving between villages
- Combat — fight feral vampires who resist unification
- Diplomacy — convince clan leaders to join the Covenant
- Introduce the "human authority" threat — hunters, militia

**Enemies:**
- Feral vampires (wild, aggressive)
- Human hunters (basic, suspicious)
- Human militia (organized, dangerous)

**Objectives:**
1. Locate the first clan in the eastern village
2. Convince or defeat the clan leader
3. Move to the western village (avoid hunters)
4. Unite the second clan
5. Return to the vault for the oath ceremony

**Cutscene:** The clans gather. Acheron speaks. The oath is sworn. The Crimson Covenant is born.

---

#### Mission 3: Blood Ritual
**Song:** Blood Ritual
**Setting:** Hidden Vaults
**Objective:** Perform the first Planar Siphon, start the First Leak

**Narrative:**
Acheron uses the Tome to perform the first Planar Siphon — banking human life-frequencies. The ritual begins the First Leak of souls. The Void stirs.

**Gameplay:**
- Stealth — avoid human authorities who sense something wrong
- Ritual sequence — perform the siphon (puzzle/mechanic)
- First appearance of shadow creatures — attracted by the Leak
- Combat — defend the ritual site

**Enemies:**
- Human authorities (investigating the disturbance)
- Shadow creatures (first appearance, attracted by the Leak)
- Corrupted spirits (ethereal, hard to hit)

**Objectives:**
1. Prepare the ritual site
2. Defend against human investigators
3. Complete the Planar Siphon
4. Repel the shadow creatures attracted by the Leak

**Cutscene:** The ritual completes. Souls flow into the Tome. The Void stirs in the darkness. Acheron feels the power — and the danger.

---

#### Mission 4: Phantom Realm
**Song:** Phantom Realm
**Setting:** Eldorian Cities
**Objective:** Infiltrate human politics, become invisible kings

**Narrative:**
The Covenant enters its "Mafia Phase." Acheron's vampires infiltrate human society — merchants, nobles, guards. They become invisible, predatory kings.

**Gameplay:**
- Stealth-focused — disguise, infiltration, social manipulation
- Multiple approaches — bribe, threaten, charm, or fight
- Establish safe houses, bribed officials, controlled businesses
- First appearance of human resistance (small, disorganized)

**Enemies:**
- Human authorities (corrupt, can be bribed)
- Human resistance (small groups, suspicious)
- Rival supernatural creatures (territorial)

**Objectives:**
1. Infiltrate the noble's ball (disguise)
2. Bribe or threaten the city guard captain
3. Establish a safe house in the merchant district
4. Eliminate a rival supernatural creature

**Cutscene:** The city is under control. Acheron watches from the shadows. The empire grows. But the shadows grow deeper too.

---

### Phase 2: The Empire (Songs 5-7)

---

#### Mission 5: War of the Ancients
**Song:** War of the Ancients
**Setting:** Eldoria — multiple locations
**Objective:** Shadow war against the original supernatural protectors

**Narrative:**
The original supernatural protectors of Eldoria — spirits, guardians, ancient beings — resist the Covenant's expansion. A shadow war erupts.

**Gameplay:**
- Mixed stealth and combat
- Guerrilla warfare — ambush, retreat, ambush again
- Fight against diverse supernatural enemies
- Moral choices — destroy or recruit?

**Enemies:**
- Forest Spirits (fast, magical)
- Stone Guardians (slow, powerful)
- Ancient Wardens (strategic, tactical)
- Human allies (recruited vampires, loyal)

**Objectives:**
1. Locate the first guardian stronghold
2. Ambush and eliminate the forest spirits
3. Fight through the stone guardians
4. Defeat the Ancient Warden (boss)
5. Claim the territory

**Boss: The Ancient Warden**
- **Health:** High
- **Damage:** High
- **Abilities:** Nature magic, summon allies, area denial
- **Strategy:** Use Aether Sight to find weaknesses, isolate from allies, strike during vulnerability

**Cutscene:** The war ends. The Covenant is victorious. The original protectors are destroyed. Eldoria is theirs. But the victory feels hollow — something has been lost.

---

#### Mission 6: Age of Shadows
**Song:** Age of Shadows
**Setting:** Eldorian Cities
**Objective:** Rule the hidden empire, manage the siphoned life-force

**Narrative:**
The peak of vampire rule. Eldoria grows dim as life-force is siphoned. The Covenant rules with absolute, hidden authority. Acheron is at the height of his power.

**Gameplay:**
- Strategy/management — manage territories, resources, vampires
- Defend against growing human resistance
- Investigate disturbances — the Leak is getting worse
- First signs of Nyktoros's attention

**Enemies:**
- Human resistance (growing, organized)
- Shadow creatures (more numerous, aggressive)
- Corrupted vampires (losing control to the Thirst)

**Objectives:**
1. Manage three territories (assign vampire lords)
2. Defend against a resistance attack
3. Investigate the Leak (it's getting worse)
4. Contain a corrupted vampire outbreak

**Cutscene:** Acheron stands in the Heart-Citadel. The souls flow. The power grows. But the shadows are watching. Something stirs in the Void.

---

#### Mission 7: Forgotten City
**Song:** Forgotten City
**Setting:** The Heart-Citadel (Underground)
**Objective:** Build the massive underground fortress, house the Soul-Bank

**Narrative:**
Deep beneath Eldoria, Acheron builds the Heart-Citadel — a massive underground fortress to house the Soul-Bank and manage the siphoned life-energy. The construction attracts shadow creatures.

**Gameplay:**
- Underground exploration — navigate ancient tunnels
- Build and defend the Heart-Citadel
- Fight shadow creatures that infest the depths
- Discover ancient secrets beneath Eldoria

**Enemies:**
- Shadow creatures (numerous, territorial)
- Corrupted spirits (ancient, powerful)
- Tunnel beasts (massive, ambush predators)

**Objectives:**
1. Explore the ancient tunnels
2. Clear the shadow creatures
3. Establish the Heart-Citadel
4. Secure the Soul-Bank

**Cutscene:** The Heart-Citadel is complete. Acheron stands in the central chamber. The souls flow through the walls. The power is immense. But the shadows in the deep are watching.

---

### Phase 3: The Fall (Songs 8-13)

---

#### Mission 8: Rising Tide
**Song:** Rising Tide
**Setting:** Eldorian Cities
**Objective:** Human scholars notice the shadow-tax, resistance grows

**Narrative:**
A new generation of human scholars begins to notice the shadow-tax on their lives. Secret resistance groups form. The Covenant's grip weakens.

**Gameplay:**
- Stealth — avoid growing resistance patrols
- Investigate — find the resistance leaders
- Combat — small skirmishes, ambushes
- Moral choices — crush the resistance or negotiate?

**Enemies:**
- Human resistance (organized, determined)
- Human scholars (non-combatants, targets)
- Sympathetic vampires (defecting to humans)

**Objectives:**
1. Locate the resistance cell
2. Interrogate a captured rebel
3. Disrupt a resistance meeting
4. Chase down a fleeing scholar

**Cutscene:** The resistance is larger than Acheron thought. They know about the Covenant. They know about the souls. The hunt begins.

---

#### Mission 9: Battle for Soul
**Song:** Battle for Soul
**Setting:** The Heart-Citadel
**Objective:** Nyktoros's attention, spiritual war to prevent Void consumption

**Narrative:**
Nyktoros has noticed the soul-banking. The Void stirs. Acheron must fight a spiritual war to prevent the Void from consuming his Covenant.

**Gameplay:**
- Spiritual combat — fight in the Aether, not the physical world
- Defend the Soul-Bank from Void incursions
- Use the Tome's power against the Void
- High stakes — failure means destruction

**Enemies:**
- Void Incarnations (manifestations of the Void)
- Corrupted souls (siphoned, now hostile)
- Nyktoros's Herald (mini-boss)

**Objectives:**
1. Enter the Aether
2. Defend the Soul-Bank from three waves
3. Defeat the Void Incarnations
4. Repel Nyktoros's Herald

**Boss: Nyktoros's Herald**
- **Health:** High
- **Damage:** Very High
- **Abilities:** Void magic, soul drain, fear aura
- **Strategy:** Use the Tome's power to weaken, strike during vulnerability, avoid soul drain

**Cutscene:** The Herald is repelled. But Nyktoros has seen the Soul-Bank. The Void will return. Acheron realizes he has attracted something he cannot control.

---

#### Mission 10: Fall of the Covenant
**Song:** Fall of the Covenant
**Setting:** The Heart-Citadel
**Objective:** Disband the Covenant, order the vampires to hide

**Narrative:**
Realizing the golden age is over, Acheron orders the subterranean capital abandoned. The Covenant disperses into isolated cells across Eldoria to avoid attracting Nyktoros's gaze. The empire is dismantled.

**Gameplay:**
- Evacuate the Heart-Citadel — pack or destroy sensitive materials
- Send vampires to their new hiding places
- Defend against a final resistance attack during the evacuation
- Seal the vaults — the Soul-Bank remains, but the Citadel is abandoned

**Enemies:**
- Human resistance (opportunistic, attacking during the evacuation)
- Corrupted vampires (feral, attacking anyone)
- Shadow creatures (drawn to the chaos)

**Objectives:**
1. Pack the Heart-Citadel's records and artifacts
2. Send vampires to three safe houses
3. Defend the evacuation from resistance attack
4. Seal the vaults and abandon the Citadel

**Cutscene:** The Heart-Citadel goes dark. Acheron walks the empty halls one last time. The souls still flow through the walls, but the empire is gone. He orders the vampires to break into small cells, hide, blend in with humans. "Lest Nyktoros detect our glow."

---

#### Mission 11: Scattered Remnants
**Song:** Scattered Remnants
**Setting:** Eldoria — scattered locations
**Objective:** Vampires adapt to hiding, Acheron hears about Malakar

**Narrative:**
The vampires adapt to living as clandestine secret societies in Eldoria. Meanwhile, rumors reach Acheron that a young Nythorian scholar named Malakar is seeking ancient dark lore.

**Gameplay:**
- Stealth-focused — move between hidden vampire cells
- Investigate — who is Malakar? What does he want?
- Minimal combat — survival, not conquest
- Build tension — Malakar is a threat, but not yet immediate

**Enemies:**
- Human authorities (suspicious, investigating)
- Corrupted vampires (feral, dangerous)

**Objectives:**
1. Visit three vampire cells to ensure they're safe
2. Hear rumors about a Nythorian scholar
3. Learn about Malakar's search for ancient lore
4. Decide: seek out this scholar or let him be?

**Cutscene:** Acheron stands in the shadows, listening to reports. A scholar in Nythoria is searching for something ancient. The name Malakar surfaces. The scholar is looking for forbidden knowledge. Acheron feels a chill — the name means nothing, but the ambition means everything.

---

#### Mission 12: The Curse
**Song:** The Curse
**Setting:** Eldoria — deep underground
**Objective:** Acheron reflects on the Thirst, realizes the curse is permanent

**Narrative:**
Acheron reflects on the permanence of the Thirst. He realizes that by attempting to transcend his Aurorian exile, he permanently cursed his lineage to be eternal parasites. The curse cannot be undone.

**Gameplay:**
- Introspective mission — minimal combat, focus on narrative
- Navigate through memory sequences — see the first transformation, the first feeding
- Confront corrupted vampires — see what the Thirst does without control
- Decide: accept the curse or try to undo it (you can't)

**Enemies:**
- Corrupted vampires (examples of the curse's worst outcome)
- Shadow creatures (drawn to Acheron's emotional state)
- Memory enemies (symbolic, not real)

**Objectives:**
1. Navigate the memory sequences
2. Confront a corrupted vampire
3. Accept the curse (narrative choice)
4. Decide to hide the Tome

**Cutscene:** Acheron kneels in the darkness. The Thirst pulses in his veins. He cannot undo what he has done. But he can prevent it from getting worse. The Tome must be hidden. He will carry it to Nythoria and cast it into the deep.

---

#### Mission 13: Beyond Horizon
**Song:** Beyond Horizon
**Setting:** The Deep Roads → The Black Well (Nythoria)
**Objective:** Acheron discards the Forbidden Tome at the Black Well

**Narrative:**
Acheron journeys to the subterranean abyss of Nythoria and discards the Forbidden Tome into the ancient ruins (~100 BGD). He believes it is safely lost forever, directly setting up the start of *Vows of Silence* where Malakar finds it.

**Gameplay:**
- Stealth — avoid corrupted vampires and shadow creatures
- Underground navigation — ancient tunnels, dangerous terrain
- Final confrontation — a last stand against corrupted vampires
- Hide the Tome at the Black Well

**Enemies:**
- Corrupted vampires (feral, attacking anyone)
- Shadow creatures (drawn to the Tome's power)
- Tunnel beasts (massive, territorial)

**Objectives:**
1. Travel through the Deep Roads to Nythoria
2. Navigate the underground tunnels
3. Find the Black Well
4. Defend against corrupted vampires
5. Cast the Tome into the well

**Cutscene:** The Tome falls into the Black Well. It sinks into the darkness. Acheron watches it disappear. The Void stirs. The future is set. The Tome will be found. Malakar will claim it. The cycle will continue. But Acheron has bought time — not much, but some. He turns away and walks into the shadows. The Covenant is over. The curse remains. But the book is hidden. For now.

**Ending:** Acheron walks away from the Black Well. The camera holds on the well. Deep below, the Tome waits. The screen fades to black. Title card: *Vows of Silence — Coming Soon*

## 8. Connection to Other Games

**Sequel:** *Vows of Silence* — Malakar finds the Tome, rises to power, and destroys Acheron's physical body (The Threshold Assassination). That game covers ~100 to 0 BGD.

**Seeds:**
- The Forbidden Tome → Malakar finds it → The Great Darkness
- Acheron's bloodline → Kael's ancestor → The Living Lock
- The Thirst → vampire curse → Beyond the Veil of Twilight

**Note:** Malakar is mentioned in this game (Song 11: Scattered Remnants — Acheron hears about a Nythorian scholar seeking ancient lore), but he never appears. He is the protagonist of *Vows of Silence*.

**The Cycle:**
1. Acheron creates the vampires (Crimson Covenant)
2. The Covenant falls (Crimson Covenant)
3. The Tome is hidden (Crimson Covenant)
4. Malakar finds it (Vows of Silence)
5. Malakar destroys Acheron (Vows of Silence)
6. The Great Darkness (A Tapestry of Souls)
7. Nyktoros is sealed (A Tapestry of Souls)
8. Kael is born (Beyond the Veil of Twilight)
9. The Lock fails (Eldoria's Prophecy)
10. Acheron returns (future story)

## 9. Development Notes

### Priority Order
1. **Phase 1: The Rise** (Missions 1-4) — core gameplay, introduce mechanics
2. **Phase 2: The Empire** (Missions 5-7) — strategy, underground, boss fights
3. **Phase 3: The Fall** (Missions 8-13) — tragedy, disbanding, curse, hiding the book

### Album Integration
- Each mission is tied to a song
- Music plays during key moments
- Lyrics appear in cutscenes (subtle, not explicit)
- The album is the narrative backbone

### Tone
- Dark, predatory, tragic
- Acheron is not a hero — he's a tyrant, a visionary, and a prisoner of his own creation
- The player experiences the rise and fall of an empire
- The ending is not happy — Acheron hides the Tome and walks away, knowing it will be found
- The true horror comes later, in *Vows of Silence*
