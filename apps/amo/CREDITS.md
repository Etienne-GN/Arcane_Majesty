# Art Credits

Third-party art assets used in Arcane Majesty Online, their sources, and
their license terms — verified directly against each publisher's own
license page, not assumed. This file is the source of record for the
in-game Credits screen (`src/scenes/CreditsScene.js`).

autotile tilesets (`SampleMap/`, `pipoya_autotiles_type1/2/3[_static]/`).

- Source: [pipoya.itch.io](https://pipoya.itch.io/) / [pipoya.net](https://pipoya.net/)
