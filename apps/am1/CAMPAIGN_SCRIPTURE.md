# AC1: Eldoria's Prophecy - Campaign Scripture (Strict Lore Edition)

**Status:** Director's Master Script for Claude (Coder)
**Source Material:** *Eldoria's Prophecy* (Era III Mid, ~500 AGD)
**Mandate:** Strict adherence to Album Plot and Lyrics.

---

## Act I: The Call of the Architect

### Track 1: Echoes of Stone (The Tower)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin discovers the "Gap" in history and geological anomalies.
*   **Quest:** "The Unreadable Record"
    *   **Objective:** Search the Archives for mentions of 0 GD.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"The Great Darkness, stories half-told." Eldrin realizes the written history of the world is a lie or incomplete.
    *   **Gameplay:** Investigation of the Tower’s basement strata reveals the "Echoes" (spectral ripples in the ground).

### Track 2: Dreamweaver’s Call (The Vision)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Vorgos (The Stormbringer) pulls Eldrin into a vision of 900 AGD.
*   **Quest:** "Summons from the Deep"
    *   **Objective:** Survive the "Rhythm of a Cosmic Rhyme" (Wave survival).
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"I see a sky of violet and blood / A Star-Guard dying in the rising flood." Vorgos commands Eldrin to find the **Anchor** (Heart Stone).
    *   **Ending:** Eldrin wakes up with the coordinates burned into his mind.

### Track 3: Odyssey’s Dawn (The Journey Begins)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin leaves his life as a scholar.
*   **Quest:** "The Solitary Way"
    *   **Objective:** Exit the Tower and travel through the neutral villages.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"The scholar's life is mine to live no more... They do not know the reason why I fly."
    *   **Interaction:** NPCs in the village speak of simple things, highlighting their ignorance of the coming breach.

---

## Act II: Trials and Tribulations

### Track 4: Summit of Despair (The Ascent)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Climbing the treacherous mountain pass; the onset of doubt.
*   **Quest:** "The Icy Crown"
    *   **Objective:** Reach the peak of the mountain during a mana-storm.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"I climb for those who are not born... Finding comfort in this empty space."
    *   **Boss (Director's Directive):** **The Whisperer of Doubt (General Malphas)**. He exploits the "Frozen tears" and solitude mentioned in the lyrics to slow the player.

### Track 5: Sylvan Sanctuary (The Hidden Forest)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Finding a valley of magic amidst the snow.
*   **Quest:** "The Sacred Trust"
    *   **Objective:** Pass the trial of the Elemental guarding the valley.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"A wise old soul, a guiding light... In the sanctuary, day turns to night."
    *   **Interaction:** Speak with **The Hermit**. He provides the knowledge required to understand the Heart Stone's purpose as a "Contingency."

### Track 6: Treachery’s Bite (The Betrayal)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin is betrayed by his traveler companion.
*   **Quest:** "The Sudden Sting"
    *   **Objective:** Escort the Companion to the Fire Gate.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"He seeks the Stone to break the seal / A wound that time will never heal."
    *   **Event:** The Companion strikes, revealing they are an agent of the Umbral Legion. Boss fight against the **Corrupted Companion**.

### Track 7: The Solitary Path (Isolation)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin accepts his role as a lone guardian.
*   **Quest:** "Until the Bitter End"
    *   **Objective:** Cross the volcanic ridge alone.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"No friends to lose, no trust to break / I do this for the future's sake."
    *   **Atmosphere:** High Mana Scent; enemies are more aggressive to emphasize Eldrin's vulnerability.

### Track 8: Inferno's Trial (The Crucible)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Navigating the labyrinth of flames.
*   **Quest:** "The Burning Gate"
    *   **Objective:** Overcome the fiery puzzles and survive the heat.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"It burns the fear, it burns the doubt / And lets the inner fire out."
    *   **Boss (Director's Directive):** **The Pyre-Lord (General Xarathos)**. He represents the "agony" Eldrin must endure to be "forged" for his destiny.

---

## Act III: The Heartstone’s Legacy

### Track 9: Eldoria's Heartbeat (The Discovery)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Entering the ancient ruins to find the artifact.
*   **Quest:** "The Cosmic Seed"
    *   **Objective:** Locate the Heart Stone of Creation.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"The ancient ruins hum a tune... The Heart Stone's magic starts to flow."
    *   **Gameplay:** First use of the Heart Stone to "Witness" a ghostly reconstruction of Eldoria’s golden age.

### Track 10: Heart of War (The Final Stand)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Defending the artifact from the shadow entity.
*   **Quest:** "The Battle’s Cry"
    *   **Objective:** Final boss fight against the **Silent Executioner (General Voraun)**.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"I force the shadow entity to kneel... Legends carved in time's embrace."
    *   **Mechanic:** Must use "Aetheric Sight" to predict the General's teleportation strikes.

### Track 11: The Weight of Eternity (The Fate)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin realizes victory means isolation.
*   **Quest:** "The Heavy Crown"
    *   **Objective:** Reach the Tower of Archives while carrying the Stone.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"A hundred years, and then three more / To wait behind the iron door."
    *   **Interaction:** Vorgos appears one last time to confirm the burden.

### Track 12: Dawn’s Embrace (The Epilogue)
*   **Narrative Anchor:** Eldrin begins his watch as the "Blind Prophet."
*   **Quest:** "The Silent Vow"
    *   **Objective:** Lock the Tower for the final time.
    *   **Lore Beat:** Found in lyrics—"I am the watcher, here and now / Until the Star-Guard needs the key."
    *   **Ending Scene:** Eldrin sitting in the tower, eyes clouded (Blind Prophet), watching the seasons change in a time-lapse.
