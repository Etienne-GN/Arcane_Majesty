# AC1: Eldoria's Prophecy - Game Scenario & Lore Directive

**Status:** Lore-Master Directive for Claude (Coder)
**Source Material:** *Eldoria's Prophecy* (Era III Mid, ~500 AGD)
**Protagonist:** Eldrin Nightshade (The Blind Prophet)

## 1. Narrative Core (The "Lore Anchor")

This game is **not** a generic fantasy quest. It is a **Cosmic Contingency Operation** orchestrated by **Vorgos the Stormbringer**.

*   **The Conflict:** 200 years ago, Kael became the "Living Lock." The Void is slowly leaking, and the resonance is killing Kael. If he fails, the universe ends in ~900 AGD.
*   **The Mission:** Eldrin is activated in 500 AGD to find the **Heart Stone of Creation**. This artifact is a "Cosmic Insulator." Eldrin's job is to secure it so it can be used 400 years later to stabilize the Lock.
*   **The Tragedy:** Eldrin must accept that he will never see the world saved. He is the bridge. He must become an immortal, solitary guardian, waiting in silence for four centuries.

## 2. Gameplay & Scenario Mapping

### Prologue: The Tower of Archives (Echoes of Stone)
*   **Goal:** Tutorial & World Building.
*   **Atmosphere:** Dusty, quiet, scholarly.
*   **Event:** Eldrin finds the "Gap" in the archives. Interaction with books/scrolls triggers the first vision.
*   **Lore Note:** The "Echoes" are literal. Use sound design or visual ripples to show that Eldrin senses the world "breaking" before anyone else.

### Level 1: The Vision Rift (Dreamweaver’s Call)
*   **Goal:** Introduction to Combat & Magic.
*   **Setting:** A surreal, distorted version of the world (900 AGD).
*   **Event:** Vorgos (The Stormbringer) appears as a Giant Eye/Storm. He shows Eldrin the "Violet Sky."
*   **Mechanic:** Eldrin receives the "Compass of the Storm"—a HUD element or magic item that points toward the Heart Stone.

### Level 2: The Path to the Summit (Odyssey’s Dawn / Summit of Despair)
*   **Goal:** Exploration & Survival.
*   **Setting:** Snowy mountain passes.
*   **Enemy Type:** Corrupted wildlife, "Shadow-touched" remnants.
*   **Scenario:** Eldrin is pursued by a "Companion" (NPC) who seems helpful but is secretly looking for the Stone's power for themselves.

### Level 3: The Sylvan Sanctuary (The Hidden Forest)
*   **Goal:** Skill Unlocks & Lore Deep-Dive.
*   **Setting:** A vibrant, magical forest that contrasts with the cold mountains.
*   **Key NPC:** The Hermit (a former Guardian or a projection of Vorgos).
*   **Unlock:** Eldrin learns "Aether Sight" (allows seeing hidden paths/items).

### Level 4: The betrayal (Treachery’s Bite)
*   **Goal:** Narrative Twist & Boss Fight.
*   **Setting:** The entrance to the Fire Labyrinth.
*   **Event:** The companion strikes. Eldrin is left for dead/debuffed.
*   **Boss:** The Corrupted Companion.

### Level 5: The Labyrinth of Flames (Inferno’s Trial)
*   **Goal:** Dungeon Crawling / Puzzle Solving.
*   **Setting:** Magma-filled ruins.
*   **Lore Note:** This is where Eldrin proves he is "unbreakable." The fire burns away his doubt.

### Level 6: The Heart of Eldoria (Eldoria's Heartbeat)
*   **Goal:** The Search for the Artifact.
*   **Setting:** High-fantasy ancient ruins, floating stones, humming energy.
*   **Event:** Eldrin finds the Heart Stone. It pulses with the "Heartbeat" of the world.

### Level 7: The Void Guardian (Heart of War)
*   **Goal:** Final Boss.
*   **Boss:** A "Void-Echo"—a manifestation of the leak that has been tracking the Stone.
*   **Mechanic:** Must use the Heart Stone’s energy to shield against Void attacks.

### Epilogue: The Weight of Eternity
*   **Goal:** Narrative Resolution.
*   **Event:** Vorgos appears. He doesn't take the stone. He tells Eldrin: "Now, you wait."
*   **Visual:** Eldrin returns to his tower (or a new sanctuary). The game ends with a montage of seasons changing rapidly while Eldrin remains motionless, guarding the Stone.

## 3. Technical Directives for Claude

1.  **Vorgos's Presence:** The Stormbringer should be a recurring visual/audio motif (thunder, violet flashes).
2.  **The Compass:** The `Compass of the Storm` should be the primary quest tracker.
3.  **Magic Style:** Eldrin’s magic is "Scholar-Magic"—runic, geometric, and blue/violet in color (Aether-based). Refer to the full documentation suite (`magic_system.md`, `combat_system.md`, `equipment_tiers.md`, `alchemy_and_healing.md`, `economy_and_inventory.md`, `investigation_and_dialogue.md`, `audio_design.md`) for the complete RPG framework.
4.  **World State:** The world in 500 AGD is "The Silent Century." It's peaceful but "wrong." NPCs should be oblivious to the cosmic threat. Magic use here is a "Lighthouse" that attracts predators. Refer to `data/lore/bestiary.md` for enemy types and the "Ping" system.
5.  **The Endings:** We are sticking to the **True Ending (Restored Balance/Eternal Guard)**. Eldrin accepts the burden.
