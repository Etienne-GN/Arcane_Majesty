# Arcane Majesty: Alchemy, Crafting, and Healing Resonance

This document defines the restorative and constructive sciences of the Arcane Majesty universe, focusing on how Eldrin Nightshade sustains himself during his long journey.

## 1. Healing Magic: "The Stabilization Principle"
In Arcane Majesty, magic cannot "create" life or flesh out of nothing. Healing is the act of using **Aurorian Resonance** (High-Frequency) to stabilize a biological form and accelerate its natural recovery.

*   **Restoration (Tier I):** Closes minor wounds and stops bleeding. Requires low concentration.
*   **Purification (Tier II):** Removes "Void-Corruption" or "Shadow-Touch" from the soul/body. Essential for surviving encounters with the Umbral Legion.
*   **Aetheric Mending (Tier III):** Reattaches limbs or heals internal organ failure. Extremely mana-intensive and requires long "Structuring" times (Vulnerable state).

**The Cost of Healing:** Healing someone else drains Eldrin’s Mana Pool significantly. Self-healing is easier but causes "Resonance Fatigue," making subsequent spells weaker for a short time.

## 2. Gathering & Crafting: The Scholar's Necessities
Alchemy and Cooking are the primary methods Eldrin uses to sustain his resonance.

### **A. The Tools of the Trade**
*   **The Iron Axe:** Required to gather **Wood** from trees. Essential for campfires.
*   **The Iron Pickaxe:** Required to mine **Minerals** and **Aether-Shards** from stone veins.
*   **Manual Gathering:** Herbs, mushrooms, and animal reagents (meat/pelt) are gathered without tools.

### **B. Crafting Specializations**
*   **Alchemy (The Alembic):** 
    *   *Potions:* Aether-Draughts (Mana), Vitality Salves (HP).
    *   *Oils:* Augmentation oils for weapons.
*   **Cooking (The Campfire):**
    *   *Food:* Roasting meat or boiling herbs to create meals that provide long-term buffs (e.g., +Stamina for 5 minutes).

### **C. Limits**
*   **No Textiles:** Eldrin does not craft cloth, leather gear, or armor. These must be found in ruins (Relics) or purchased from the Archivist's Guild.

---

## 3. The Foundry & Campfire Logic
*   **The Portable Alembic:** Used for Alchemy anywhere.
*   **The Campfire:** Requires Wood. Used for Cooking and temporary saving.
*   **The Foundry of the Ancients:** Used for permanent gear upgrades (Tier I -> IV).

---

## 4. Technical Directives for Claude (AC1)

1.  **Gathering System:** Implement "Interaction Nodes" in the world (bushes, rocks, monster corpses) that drop reagents.
2.  **Crafting UI:** A sub-menu in the Inventory where players select a recipe and "Hold to Craft" (casting time).
3.  **Healing Spell Mechanics:** 
    *   Healing is a "Channeled" spell. Eldrin must stand still.
    *   Visuals: Golden/White light (Aurorian) to contrast with his usual Blue/Violet (Aetheric) spells.
4.  **Consumable Quick-Slots:** A dedicated HUD area for quick-using Aether-Draughts or Vitality Salves during combat.
