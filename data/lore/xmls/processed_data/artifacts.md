# Artifacts of Arcane Majesty (Linear Era: 0 GD)

This document defines the primary artifacts of power established or discovered during the era of "A Tapestry of Souls."

### The Forbidden Tome
- **Nature:** An ancient, sentient-shadow record of Void energy patterns.
- **Role:** The Blueprints of the Void.
- **History:** 
    - **Acheron’s Era:** Discovered by Acheron in Eldoria; used to harvest dormant Void energy and create the first vampires.
    - **Malakar’s Era (Foreshadowing):** Awaits discovery by the young sorcerer Malakar in the ruins of Nythoria.
- **Power:** Grants the wielder the ability to manipulate shadow-frequency and siphon Void energy.

### The Crystal Focus
- **Nature:** A massive, multi-faceted lens of solidified starlight.
- **Role:** The Sentinel’s Eye.
- **Location:** The Seer's Library in Auroria.
- **Power:** Allows the **Seers of Auroria** to monitor the energy frequencies of the three parallel planes.

### The Heartstone of Creation
- **Nature:** The physical anchor of the Material Plane's life-frequency.
- **Role:** The Core of Eldoria.
- **Status:** Lost to history during the 0 GD era; mentioned in prophecies as the only object capable of healing a plane ravaged by the Void.
