# Races of Arcane Majesty

This document defines the primary races inhabiting the realms, refined for the Neo4j graph and narrative consistency.

## Elven Lineages

### Aurorian (High Elf of Light)
- **Nature:** Elven beings of pure light and divine energy.
- **Longevity:** Millennia-spanning lifespans.
- **Traits:** Ethereal appearance, imbued with divine power that manifests as radiant magic. They are the natural antithesis to the Void.
- **Representative:** Anya.

### Nythorian (Night Elf of the Underworld)
- **Nature:** Elven kin residing in the Underworld and its border-realms (Nythoria/Ravenspire).
- **Longevity:** Long-lived, though battle-hardened by eternal conflict.
- **Traits:** Imbued with the power of darkness and shadow. They are physically resilient and spiritually anchored to the deeper realms.
- **Representatives:** Seraphina, Malakar, Valen, Draven.

## Human Lineages

### Human
- **Nature:** The mortal inhabitants of Eldoria and surrounding lands.
- **Longevity:** Standard mortal lifespans.
- **Traits:** Highly adaptable with varying degrees of magical affinity.
- **Representative:** Kael (before the curse).

### Storm-Touched (Unique Human)
- **Nature:** A human imbued with a shard of the **Stormbringer essence**.
- **Traits:** Exceptional longevity and physical resistance over average humans.
- **Power Type:** **Linear growth**. Unlike static elven power, the Storm-Touched have no known ceiling; their magical capacity expands exponentially with training.
- **Representative:** Eldrin Nightshade.

## Ancient Entities

Primordial beings who predate the elven and human races, tied to fundamental cosmic forces.

### Nyktoros (The Void)
- **Dominion:** Perpetual darkness, silence, and the unmaking of reality.

### Elara (The Starlight)
- **Dominion:** Cosmic knowledge, time, and the guidance of fate.

### The Stormbringer (The Chaos)
- **Dominion:** Raw atmospheric power, rifts in reality, and the force of change. 
- **Note:** Shards of this entity's essence can imbue mortals, creating the "Storm-Touched."
