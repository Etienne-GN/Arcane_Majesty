# Races of Arcane Majesty (Linear Era: 0 GD)

This document defines the primary races established during the era of "A Tapestry of Souls."

## Elven Lineages

### Aurorian (High Elf of Light)
- **Nature:** Elven beings of pure light and divine energy.
- **Traits:** Ethereal appearance, imbued with radiant magic. The natural antithesis to the Void.
- **Home:** Auroria and the Celestial Gardens.
- **Representative:** Anya.

### Nythorian (Night Elf of the Underworld)
- **Nature:** Elven kin residing in the Underworld, battle-hardened by proximity to the Void.
- **Traits:** Imbued with the power of darkness and shadow. They are physically resilient and spiritually anchored to the deeper realms.
- **Representatives:** Seraphina, Valen.

## Ancient Entities

Primordial beings who predate the elven races, tied to fundamental cosmic forces.

### Nyktoros (The Void)
- **Dominion:** Perpetual darkness, silence, and the unmaking of reality.

### Elara (The Starlight)
- **Dominion:** Cosmic knowledge, time, and the guidance of fate.

### Aetheros (The Stormbringer)
- **Dominion:** Raw atmospheric power, rifts in reality, and the force of change.
