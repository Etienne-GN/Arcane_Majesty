# Prophecies of Arcane Majesty (Linear Era: 0 GD)

This document defines the core foretellings and cosmic laws revealed during the era of "A Tapestry of Souls."

### The Void-Echo (The Impossible Key)
- **Revealed In:** *Shadows and Stardust* (Song 7)
- **Content:** "Born of the Eclipse, spirit-stained by the Silence, one shall walk where Light is unmade."
- **Meaning:** Predicts the birth of a being immune to Nyktoros’s magic.
- **Subject:** **Anya**.

### The Living Lock
- **Revealed In:** *Shadows and Stardust* (Song 7)
- **Content:** "The Void cannot be unmade, only contained. The Warden shall weave a prison of infinite shadow, anchored by their own life."
- **Meaning:** Establishes that Nyktoros must be sealed rather than destroyed, and that the warden must remain with him.

### The Recombination (Acheron’s Secret)
- **Revealed In:** Forbidden research within the Seer's archives (Exposed by Elara).
- **Content:** The theory that merging pure Aurorian Light with Raw Void energy creates a transcendent godhood.
- **Meaning:** This is the "Secret Prophecy" that drives Acheron's desire to achieve godhood through the Cosmic Harvest.
