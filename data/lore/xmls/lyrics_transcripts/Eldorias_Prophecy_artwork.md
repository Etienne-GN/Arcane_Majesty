# Eldoria's Prophecy — Artwork Descriptions

12 song-specific artwork prompts for *Eldoria's Prophecy* (~500 AGD), the story of Eldrin Nightshade, master mage and scholar-historian of Thaloria.

**Format rules**
- Square 1:1 (album/single covers).
- No text in the image (titles are added in post).
- Each song is one chapter of the album's story — each prompt is a standalone scene.

**Character consistency**
- Canonical character visuals already exist for the main characters. When the generator supports reference images (e.g. Midjourney `--cref`, Flux reference), attach the existing character art for **Eldrin**, **Vorgos**, **Anya**, and **Xarathos** to lock face/outfit continuity across all 12 covers.
- If no reference image is available, use the short canonical descriptions below.

---

## Style anchor — prepend to every prompt

> Painterly dark-fantasy album artwork, cinematic volumetric lighting, fine detail, subtle film grain, 1:1 square, no text. Palette anchors: deep slate-indigo and charcoal, warm candle-gold highlights.

---

## Characters (canonical descriptions)

- **Eldrin Nightshade** — weathered master mage, silver-grey beard, deep-blue robes with faint embroidered star-marks, wooden staff, leather journals bound to his belt.
- **Vorgos (the Stormbringer)** — colossal god of grey storm-cloud, timeless, eyes of distant lightning; Sentinel of the Aether, steps out of time itself.
- **Anya** — the Eternal Warden, ethereal scholar of the Crystal Sanctum; crystalline, grief-stricken.
- **Oren** — trusted companion, thirty years a lie; traveler's clothes, blade hidden, face half in shadow.
- **Xarathos** — the Pyre-Lord, Fallen Aurorian; figure wreathed in living flame, crown of embers, molten gaze.
- **Voraun** — the veiled antagonist; shadowed, masked, patient.

## 1. Echoes of Stone — Introspective, Atmospheric

A hooded mage-scholar (Eldrin) seated at a cluttered oak desk in a high stone tower study at night. A single candle flame lights drifting dust motes as he holds a fractured stone core that glows faintly. The floor's stone strata split with veins of pale fissure-light, exposing ancient geological lines like a scarred timeline of the world. Rolled parchments and tomes crowd the shelves; a narrow window overlooks a dark river town far below (Silverrun). Mood: quiet dread of a buried, unbearable history. Warm candle-gold against cool slate-blue, shallow depth of field, volumetric dust.

## 2. Dreamweaver's Call — Ethereal, Terrifying

Eldrin's tiny figure floats suspended inside a colossal violet sky that cracks like glass — a great luminous fissure tearing across the heavens as the Star-Guard shatters. Behind him looms a towering storm-god of grey cloud (Vorgos), eyes like distant lightning. Far below on a shattered plain, a weeping woman (Anya) cradles a broken armored man; fragments of a dying star fall like burning tears. Palette: violets, deep indigo, piercing white fissure light. Cosmic scale, eldritch awe.

## 3. Odyssey's Dawn — Adventurous, Resolute

Eldrin stands at the threshold of his tower door, staff in hand, hood thrown back, one foot already over the lintel. Dawn breaks over a misty, reborn land — distant towns, roads, and forest spread below the high keep. Warm rose-gold sunrise against cool morning blue; journals at his belt, determination in his stance. Low-angle hero shot: the figure fills the left third, a vast open world opening to the right. First step of the Odyssey.

## 4. Summit of Despair — Melancholic, Powerful

A lone hooded figure (Eldrin) climbs a razor-sharp ridge in thin air, wind tearing at his robes, breath misting in the cold. Empty, monumental mountains fall away beneath him; a pale dead sky; a single raven passes. Palette drained to cold slate, white, and grey-blue. Tiny figure against vast emptiness, high-contrast negative space, snow-scoured rock. Doubt and resolve in equal weight.

## 5. Sylvan Sanctuary — Mystical, Enchanting

An impossible hidden valley in early spring — towering trees whose high canopy arches like the ribs of a great green cathedral, shafts of golden-green light slanting through. Bioluminescent blossoms and luminous fungi glow at the forest floor beside a mirror-still pool. Small strange, gentle creatures watch from the leaves. Eldrin stands small among giants, wonder breaking his weariness. Palette: emerald, teal, golden light, soft drifting mist.

## 6. Treachery's Bite — Dark, Intense

At the edge of a black-glass plain where the obsidian meets an ash-line before a distant fire-gate glow. Two figures mid-freeze-frame: the companion (Oren) has struck — dagger caught in the lamplight — his mask falling as he moves; the betrayed figure (Eldrin) twists away, robe tearing. Black glass mirrors ember-orange light; the composition cuts sharp and diagonal. Palette: obsidian black, ash grey, bloodless ember orange. The moment trust dies.

## 7. The Solitary Path — Resolute, Lonely

A wounded, hooded figure (Eldrin) walks a bare stone path through a cold, featureless highland, one hand pressed to a side wound, staff used as a crutch. Low fog, a weak winter sun, his shadow long and utterly alone. No companions — only echoes of his own footprints vanishing behind him. Palette: desaturated blue-grey, silver light. Small centered figure beneath a vast blank sky.

## 8. Inferno's Trial — Dramatic, Contrasting

A maze of fire-forged labyrinth walls — black volcanic glass veined with molten magma. Before Eldrin stands a towering gate of living flame bearing the face of Xarathos, the Pyre-Lord: crown of embers, molten gaze, the gate roaring with his name. Eldrin's silhouette braces against the inferno, cool blue robes a single point of contrast. Claustrophobic corridor, towering scale. Palette: deep black, magma orange and red, one cold highlight.

## 9. Eldoria's Heartbeat — Epic, Triumphant

The Ruins of Eldoria in a red valley at dusk — colossal broken marble arches and toppled golden columns overgrown with crimson vines, the graveyard of a golden age. At the heart, a cracked obelisk beats with a warm pulse of light like a heartbeat stirring back to life. Eldrin stands small before it, wind lifting his robes. Palette: burnt red, gold leaf, warm amber glow, deep shadow. Epic scale, a lost realm reawakening.

## 10. Heart of War — Intense, Climatic

The Heartstone Chamber — a vast vault where a floating, heart-shaped crystal (the Heartstone) pulses with cold light. A figure's hand presses against its base, wreathed in burning sigils — the unworthy hand that burns. Behind, an ancient door older than the valley, covered in sealed script, cracks open with pale light spilling through. Palette: cold crystal blue-white against dark bronze, floating sparks.

## 11. The Weight of Eternity — Heavy, Burdened

Eldrin on his knees, both hands holding the Heartstone of Creation, its light seeping between his fingers and casting long shadows. A colossal storm-god of grey (Vorgos) steps out of the sky above him — timeless, eyes like storm-light — the Stone casting a planetary shadow over the valley. Palette: grey storm, cold white, a single warm amber core. The weight of forever held in two hands.

## 12. Dawn's Embrace — Hopeful, Uplifting

Eldrin, older now, stands on the parapet of his re-sealed tower at dawn, hands resting on the stone as a soft golden ward-glow shimmers around the keep. Thaloria wakes below in warm, misty light; the river Silverrun catches the rising sun. Palette: warm gold, rose, soft morning blue. Quiet triumph — the beginning of a long vigil, not an end.
