# Arcane Majesty Project Summary

**Project:** We are working on the "Arcane Majesty" fantasy universe.

**Source Material:** The lore is currently in XML files, with each file representing a music album. The songs and their lyrics are pre-existing (from Suno) and should be modified as little as possible.

**Our Task:** Our primary goal is to refine the `Plot` for each song to create a coherent, overarching narrative across all albums, starting with "A Tapestry of Souls". This album is the most challenging as it was not originally written with the larger story in mind.

**End Goal:** The refined lore will eventually be migrated to a Neo4j graph database to map out the relationships between characters, locations, and events, making the XML files obsolete.

**My Role:** I will act as your assistant to help you flesh out the story, ensure consistency, and eventually, assist with the technical task of structuring the data for the Neo4j database.
