# Arcane Majesty: Investigation and Dialogue Systems

This document defines how Eldrin Nightshade interacts with the world, uncovering the lost history of the "Great Darkness" through scholarly analysis and social interaction.

## 1. The Scholar's Eye (Investigation Mechanic)
Eldrin is not just a mage; he is a historian. The world is filled with "Echoes" of the past that only he can interpret.

*   **Aetheric Analysis:** By standing near an ancient ruin or artifact and channeling mana, Eldrin can "see" a ghostly reconstruction of its original state.
*   **Deciphering:** Eldrin can translate ancient scripts (Aurorian or Old Nythorian) found in ruins. This reveals hidden lore, quest objectives, or secret weaknesses of enemies.
*   **Chronological Mapping:** Every time Eldrin investigates a major ruin, he adds a entry to his "Master Journal," slowly filling the "Gap" in history (the missing 500 years).

---

## 2. Dialogue Tone: The Silent Century (500 AGD)
The people of Eldoria in 500 AGD are descendants of survivors who have forgotten the terror of 0 GD.

*   **Ignorant Peace:** NPCs should sound peaceful, simple, and slightly complacent. They view the Great Darkness as a "myth" or a "scary bedtime story."
*   **The "Crazy Old Man" Perception:** Eldrin is often treated with a mix of respect (as a scholar) and pity (as someone obsessed with "ancient nonsense"). 
*   **Dialogue Style:**
    *   **Commoners:** Use simple, grounded language focused on harvest, trade, and local gossip.
    *   **Scholars/Hermits:** Use more formal, cautious language. They might know *just enough* to be afraid but lack the full picture Eldrin has.
    *   **Vorgos/Eldrin:** Their dialogue should feel "weighted." Every word has cosmic significance.

---

## 3. The Archive of Souls (Lore Collection)
A system for collecting and viewing the secret history of the universe.

*   **Lore Fragments:** Found in hidden caches, ruins, or dropped by Void Generals.
    *   *Examples:* "Fragment of a Broken Crown," "Singe-Marked Diary," "Runic Shard."
*   **The Repository:** Within the game menu, the Archive allows players to read detailed accounts of Era I and Era II events.
*   **Mechanical Reward:** Collecting fragments can provide passive bonuses (e.g., "Knowledge of the Fallen" increases damage against Hollow Guards).

---

## 4. Technical Directives for Claude (AC1)

1.  **Interaction Icons:** Use a specific "Scholarly" icon (e.g., an open book or a magnifying glass) for investigation nodes.
2.  **Dialogue Trees:** Implement a system where Eldrin can ask specific "Scholarly Questions" to NPCs to unlock hidden lore or quest paths.
3.  **The Master Journal:** A dedicated UI section that acts as a codex. It should be visually presented as Eldrin's actual handwritten notes.
4.  **Ghostly Reconstructions:** Use transparent, blue/violet "Aetheric" overlays to show ruins being "repaired" during investigation sequences.
