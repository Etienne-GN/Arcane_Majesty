# Arcane Majesty: The Science of Soul-Energy & The Living Force

This document defines the mechanics of magic in the Arcane Majesty universe, reflecting a system of internal spirit and external command.

## 1. The Fuel: Soul-Energy
Magic is fueled by **Soul-Energy**, the internal "lumen" of a living being's spirit.

*   **Internal Reservoir:** Every soul generates its own energy. Through intense use during the formative years (as Eldrin did in the Tower), the capacity to hold and generate this energy can be significantly expanded.
*   **The Anchor:** Soul-Energy acts as the "Anchor" between the mind and the physical body.
*   **Expansion:** Using magic to the point of near-depletion "stretches" the soul, allowing for a larger reservoir over time.

## 2. The Medium: The Aether (The Living Force)
The Aether is the **Living Force** of the world. It is the conscious, invisible energy that permeates all things—rocks, trees, and the space between planes.

*   **Harmonization:** Casting a spell is the act of using one's **Soul-Energy** to "command" or "harmonize" with the **Living Force**.
*   **Silent Casting:** A scholar who understands the "Language of the Living Force" (Resonance) can command the Aether without spoken incantations, simply by vibrating their Soul-Energy at the correct frequency.

---

## 3. The Consequence: The Snap (Fainting)
When a mage's Soul-Energy is completely depleted, the "Anchor" to the physical body fails.

*   **Stage 1 — Fatigue:** Movement becomes sluggish as the spirit struggles to maintain physical cohesion.
*   **Stage 2 — Exhaustion:** The world turns grey. Speed drops to a crawl. Spellcasting becomes impossible.
*   **Stage 3 — The Snap:** If the mage continues to push or remains at zero energy, they lose consciousness (faint). This is a survival mechanism where the body enters "Spiritual Hibernation" to prevent the soul from detaching.

---

## 4. The Aetheric Ripples (The Scent)
Even though the fuel is internal, the act of commanding the Living Force is "loud."

*   **Ripples:** Every spell creates a vibration in the Aether. 
*   **Detection:** High-intensity commands (large spells) create ripples that can be felt by sensitive entities.
*   **Predatory Reaction:** Shadow Beasts and those attuned to the "Thirst" are drawn to these ripples, viewing the mage's Soul-Energy as a concentrated source of "Heat" (Life).
