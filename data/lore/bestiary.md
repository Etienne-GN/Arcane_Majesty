# Arcane Majesty: Bestiary of the Silent Century (~500 AGD)

In the era of Eldrin Nightshade, the world is physically peaceful, but the Aether is "thin." The Void leakage from Kael's failing lock has corrupted local wildlife and summoned the high-ranking officers of the **Legion of Souls**.

---

## 1. The Scaling Threat: The "Proximity" Rule
As Eldrin approaches the **Heart Stone of Creation**, the concentration of Aetheric Resonance increases. 
*   **Distance 100%:** Local corrupted wildlife (weak).
*   **Distance 50%:** Umbral Foot-soldiers and elemental corruption.
*   **Distance 10%:** The Void Generals and the elite Legion.

---

## 2. Regional Bestiary: The Shadow-Touched

### **A. Biome: The Whispering Woods (Prologue/Level 3)**
*   **Void-Stalker (Wolf):** Pack hunters. Blink behind player on magic hit. (Sensitivity: 1)
*   **Blight-Ent:** Ancient trees fused with Nythorian rot. Slow, heavy smash attacks. They release "Mana-Dampening Spores" when hit. (Sensitivity: 0)
*   **Root-Wraith:** Small, fast vine-creatures that entangle the player, leaving them vulnerable to Aether-Leeches. (Sensitivity: 1)

### **B. Biome: The Summit of Despair (Level 2/4)**
*   **Gloom-Beak (Great Bird):** Dive-bombs high-mana targets. Causes blindness. (Sensitivity: 2)
*   **Frost-Shade:** Invisible in snowstorms. They drain Eldrin's "Internal Heat" (Mana and HP). (Sensitivity: 1)
*   **Crag-Fiend:** Rock-skinned trolls with violet crystals growing from their backs. They act as "Resonance Rods," drawing lightning to Eldrin's location. (Sensitivity: 2)

### **C. Biome: The Inferno Labyrinth (Level 5)**
*   **Magma-Eater:** Lizard-like creatures that consume fire spells to grow larger. (Sensitivity: 2)
*   **Cinder-Soul:** Floating skulls of fire that explode into "Void-Flame" (burns both HP and Mana). (Sensitivity: 1)
*   **Obsidian Golem:** High-defense constructs. Only vulnerable when hit by a specific frequency of magic (Runic Puzzle Combat). (Sensitivity: 0)

---

## 3. The Umbral Legion (Nyktoros's Army)
These are not animals; they are the structured military of the Umbral Plane.

*   **Void-Squire:** Standard infantry. Use shadow-blades that "flicker," making them hard to parry.
*   **Aether-Leech:** Mana-draining floaters. Intercept projectiles.
*   **Hollow Guard:** Ancient armor possessed by Nythorian souls. Reflect projectiles.
*   **Void-Archer:** Fire "Siphon-Arrows" that stay lodged in Eldrin, draining mana until pulled out.

---

## 4. The Void Generals (The Harbingers of Nyktoros)
Each General guards a strategic "Resonance Node" leading to the Heart Stone.

### **General 1: Malphas, The Whisperer of Doubt**
*   **Location:** The Summit of Despair.
*   **Lore:** He feeds on the isolation of the "Blind Prophet."
*   **Combat:** Creates illusions of Eldrin's allies. The player must use "Aether Sight" to find the real General.

### **General 2: Xarathos, The Pyre-Lord**
*   **Location:** The Inferno Labyrinth.
*   **Lore:** A former Aurorian who fell to the Thirst. He controls the "Corruption of the Flame."
*   **Combat:** Floor-is-lava mechanics. He steals Eldrin's mana to fuel a "Super-Nova" attack.

### **General 3: Voraun, The Silent Executioner**
*   **Location:** The Ruins of Eldoria (Final Gate).
*   **Lore:** The tactical leader of the local Legion. He is the one who orchestrated the "Treachery's Bite" by corrupting Eldrin's companion.
*   **Combat:** A high-speed duel. He can "Parry" magic, reflecting it back into runic patterns the player must dodge.

---

## 5. Technical Directives for Claude (AC1)
1.  **Scaling Spawns:** Increase enemy density and `AetherSensitivity` levels as the player progresses through biomes.
2.  **General Mechanics:** Bosses should have "Intermission Phases" where they use lore-based dialogue to distract/debuff the player.
3.  **Regional Visuals:** Enemies in the Inferno should glow red/violet; in the Mountains, blue/violet; in the Forest, green/violet.

---
**Lore-Master Directive:** *Nyktoros does not send monsters; he sends a message. Every creature you kill is a syllable in his prophecy of the end.*
