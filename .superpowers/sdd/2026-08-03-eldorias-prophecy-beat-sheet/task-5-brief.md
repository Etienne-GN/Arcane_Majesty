## Scene beats (follow in order)

1. **The Underworld Sanctuary.** Anya watches the sealed prison of the Void; the resonance of the Lock is a constant low scream beneath the world's floor.
2. **Kael, the Living Lock, is being killed by the Lock.** The resonance erodes him from within — Vorgos said it best: it "eats the man to feed the door." Kael is stoic; Anya is angry and helpless — the anger is the shape of her love.
