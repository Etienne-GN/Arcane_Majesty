## Scene beats (from the approved plan — follow in order)

- **ch17 Eldoria's Heartbeat (~3.5k):** The Ruins of Eldoria — "ancient ruins hum a tune beneath the pale and silent moon." Ghostly reconstruction — Eldrin "Witnesses" the golden age via the Heartstone's echo (the game's Aetheric Witness mechanic in prose). The realm is a graveyard of the past; the historian sees his people's deepest history for the first time. He finds the approach to the Heartstone chamber; evidence Voraun has been here — a signature, a guarded gate; the General has held this ground longest. End: the chamber door.
- **ch18 The Pulse (~3k):** The Heartstone of Creation — "a pulse of light, a rhythm slow." Understated. A calm, stabilized resonance. Anti-climactic by design. Eldrin touches it — it accepts him (echo of ch15: unworthy hands burn). The "Heartbeat" of Eldoria syncs to his own. He understands its purpose: to hold, to stabilize, to wait. Not glory — the weight of a held breath. Voraun appears at the chamber's threshold.
- **ch19 The Silent Executioner (~4k):** Voraun — the tactical mind behind Oren's betrayal; has guarded this ground waiting for Eldrin to carry the Stone out. Confrontation: fast, silent, parries magic — a high-speed duel; runic reflection. Eldrin wins by weaponizing Voraun's need (the Stone rejects unworthy hands — Voraun can't touch it); he drives Voraun into the Stone's ward, which burns the General. Voraun's final moment: as he dies, the deeper truth — he was not fighting for the Stone's power but to *prevent* it being used, on orders from beyond the Legion. (Seed for the next saga / The Prophecy of Darkness.) End: silence. The door beyond. Something huge waits.
- **ch20 Shadow Balrog (~4k):** The final door. The Shadow Balrog — "a shadow rises from the floor, the guardian of the ancient door." Ancient, untamable, subservient to no one. It hungers for the light Eldrin holds. Eldrin tries force — fails; the Balrog is beyond physical or magical power (the Hermit's warning pays off: it cannot be commanded or bargained with). Resolution: Eldrin stops fighting. He offers the Stone's truth — not as a weapon, but as what it is: the thing that holds. He stands not as its conqueror but as its *intended* guardian. The Balrog, which exists to protect the door from misuse, cannot deny the one the Stone accepts. The Balrog yields the way — not defeated, not commanded; *satisfied*. Mastery through humility.
- **ch21 The Weight of Eternity (~3.5k):** Vorgos steps from time, "a storm of grey": "Put down the Stone, and walk away." He tests Eldrin one last time. Eldrin refuses to walk away. Vorgos reveals the true condition: he must guard the Stone for **400 years**, until a hero not yet born needs it in 900 AGD. The crushing weight: "A hundred years, and then three more." His friends will die; his world will change; he will remain. Eldrin's choice — tragic acceptance. He is the bridge between past tragedy and future salvation.
- **ch22 The Return (~3k):** The journey home with the Stone; seasons shift around him. The world of 500 AGD continues its oblivious life. He passes the places of Part I — the villages, the road where Oren joined him. What he cannot tell anyone. He is already becoming the "Blind Prophet" in spirit — blind to ordinary life, seeing only the timeline. He arrives at Thaloria. The tower stands as he left it.
- **ch23 Dawn's Embrace (~4k):** He re-seals the sanctum, locks the gate: "I am the watcher, here and now." The vigil begins. Seasons lapse in montage — the years fold. His sight remains; his life narrows to the Stone. He accepts: the cure that does nothing yet, held in the dark for a future he will never see. Close: dawn breaks; the Star-Guard (Kael) is not ready yet. Eldrin waits. "Until the Star-Guard needs the key."
