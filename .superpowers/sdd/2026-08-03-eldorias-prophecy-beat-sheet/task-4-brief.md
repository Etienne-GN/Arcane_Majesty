## Scene beats (from the approved plan — follow in order)

- **ch06 The Ascent (~3k):** Winter ascent into the mountains; Oren shares the burden, proves useful. Summit of Despair — "ice-kissed peaks, a barren expanse"; doubt creeps in. Signs of the Legion: frozen camp of corpses, a Crag-Fiend with violet crystals — someone (Malphas) ahead or claimed the pass. Oren's loyalty tested lightly; he reassures Eldrin.
- **ch07 Whisperer of Doubt (~3.5k):** Blizzard; Frost-Shades and Gloom-Beaks harass them; the Aether is thin. Malphas, the Whisperer of Doubt, reveals himself — feeds on isolation, illusions of allies and past failures. Combat via Aether Sight (seed the mechanic) + runic wards. Oren "fights beside him" but hesitates at a key moment; Malphas's illusions show Oren as betrayer, Eldrin dismisses it (dramatic irony — the vision was true). Eldrin breaks Malphas's hold; the General retreats, not killed; vows he'll have the Stone. Generals are rivals. Victory, but Eldrin is shaken.
- **ch08 Descent (~2.5k):** Survival trek down; toll on body and mind. Oren asks what the illusions showed; Eldrin lies; trust quietly cracking. Shared warmth — campfire, Oren's "lost libraries" stories — rebuilt trust; end on deceptive calm.
- **ch09 Sylvan Sanctuary (~3.5k):** "A valley green amidst the snow" — impossible early spring; forest is alive. The Elemental blocks the way: "Why do you seek the light of day?" — a trial of intent (glory vs need). Eldrin passes by speaking the truth: to prevent the catastrophe he was shown, not for himself. The forest: magical plants, strange creatures, peace. Aether Sight begins manifesting on its own.
- **ch10 The Hermit (~4k):** The hermit's hut; guide half-present, half-spirit. **The Hermit** — failed former Keeper. Story: guarded the Heartstone; the Shadow Balrog came; he was defeated and bound his spirit to the forest's Elemental to survive; the forest is his lingering will. He grants **Aether Sight** (formal mastery) and reveals the Stone is a "Cosmic Insulator" — it stabilizes, holds; not a weapon, never will be. Eldrin's purpose: guard a cure that does nothing yet. Warning: the Balrog cannot be commanded or bargained with; the only way past it is to *not be its enemy*. Foreshadow: Oren quiet, taking notes; the Hermit watches him coldly but says nothing.
- **ch11 The Fire Gate (~3k):** Departure; spring travel; air warms. Oren increasingly interested in the Stone's exact location, couched as scholar's curiosity; Eldrin shares more than he should. They reach the Fire Gate entrance — the way to the Inferno Labyrinth; the compass needle spins. Night before descent: Oren asks "What will you do when you find it?" Eldrin: guard it. Oren's smile doesn't reach his eyes.
- **ch12 Treachery's Bite (~4k):** The betrayal at the Fire Gate threshold — snow-line and ash. Oren strikes — "the dagger that the shadows bring." He seeks the Stone "to break the seal." Reveal: agent of **Voraun**, the Silent Executioner; scholarship a mask, friendship a job. Fight against corrupted Oren (Voraun's touch gives shadow-blades). Eldrin wounded, left for dead. Oren takes compass coordinates from Eldrin's notes and enters the labyrinth ahead. Eldrin survives — barely.
- **ch13 The Solitary Path (~3k):** He leaves the trusting scholar behind in the snow-line. Patches wounds, eats, plans. Vorgos's silence. The compass still points — Oren has only notes, not the real compass. The race is still on. "The solitary path, I walk alone. No friends to lose, no trust to break." He embraces isolation; descends into the Inferno Labyrinth alone.
- **ch14 Inferno's Trial (~3.5k):** Labyrinth: magma flows, "a wall of fire, a gate of flame that calls me by my given name" — the Legion seeded defenses with his identity. Puzzle-trial: Obsidian Golems require rune-frequencies; Magma-Eaters and Cinder-Souls. Uses Aether Sight + Scholar-Magic sparingly. Heat burns away doubt. End: he reaches the heart-chamber; **Xarathos** is waiting — he got there first. But Xarathos cannot take the Stone either.
- **ch15 Pyre-Lord (~3.5k):** **Xarathos**, the Pyre-Lord — fallen Aurorian corrupted by the Thirst; controls the corruption of the flame; wants the Stone to unmake the Lock, let the Void in. Why he hasn't taken it: the Stone rejects unworthy hands — touching it burns the unworthy; he needs someone the Stone *accepts* to carry it out — Eldrin. (The catch-22: all three Generals need Eldrin alive, not dead — why he keeps surviving.) Combat: crucible of flame; Xarathos steals mana for a supernova; Eldrin denies it with a rune-drain counter, finds the crack in Thirst-driven arrogance. Victory: Xarathos destroyed. Dying words: "Voraun will have you at the door. He has been waiting the longest."
- **ch16 The Crucible (~3k):** Rebirth imagery: "ashes fall, a new dawn breaks." Doubt burned out. Summer at its peak; volcanic ridge; the compass sings — the Ruins of Eldoria close. Close Part II: alone, unbreakable, the final door waits. Rising threat of Voraun.

- **Season progression:** Part II spans late winter (ch06) → spring (ch09-11) → the betrayal at snow-line/ash (ch12) → summer (ch16). Year 501 AGD.
