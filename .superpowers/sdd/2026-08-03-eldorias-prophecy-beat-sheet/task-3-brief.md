## Scene beats (follow in order)

1. **Vorgos in the Aether** — the dimension between planes. The Great Rift glows; he reads the resonance like a ledger (books, balances, overdue debts). Establish the Aether as the living force between frequencies.
2. **Kael's Lock is decaying faster than predicted.** Vorgos observes the Underworld: the Lock's resonance eroding the Star-Guard. The three Generals have caught the Heartstone's scent — three signatures converging on the ruins.
3. **He reflects on Eldrin:** not the strongest, not the chosen of prophecy — the *available* piece. "A pawn upon a cosmic board." He reviews what he has done: plucked Anya from Auroria at 0 GD, made Kael the Lock, now moved Eldrin. Each was a piece. He is not cruel; he is efficient.
4. **End beat:** Vorgos acknowledges the tragedy he is inflicting — and does it anyway. Pragmatism, not malice. A moment of something almost like regret, recognized and set aside.

## Global constraints (binding)

- **Language:** English. No AI-trope clichés ("shadows in the night," "echoes of time").
- **Tone:** Dark fantasy, mythic, pragmatic. Publishable prose — match the book's standard. Vorgos's voice is older than language, precise, patient, efficient.
- **POV:** Third-person limited on Vorgos. This is the ONLY POV for this interlude. Vorgos is an ancient entity of the Aether — not omniscient, not human; he reads resonance the way scholars read books.
- **Canon (world_lore.md):** The Aether is the dimension between planes — the conscious, invisible energy separating the three frequencies; Vorgos resides there. The Great Rift is a violent planar scar he tore at 0 GD. Anya was plucked from Auroria and thrust into the Underworld as a cosmic stop-gap; Kael is the "Living Lock" (established ~300 AGD). The three Void Generals (Malphas, Xarathos, Voraun) independently hunt the Heartstone for Nyktoros.
- **Vorgos's characterization:** pragmatic, not omniscient, not benevolent. He is the Architect of the timeline — the one who set up Anya (0 GD), Kael (~300 AGD), Eldrin (~500 AGD) as pieces.
- **Do NOT** reveal events of Part II/III. Eldrin is currently on the road (late winter 501 AGD). The Generals are converging but not yet confronted.

## Style notes

- Show, not tell. The Aether's "ledger" is metaphor he sees directly.
- The tragedy is real: he has made a monument of a man. He knows it. He proceeds.
- End on the note that seals the book's engine: the race is real, the schedule is in arrears, and Eldrin is the piece he moved.
