# Crimson Covenant — Novel

## Status: Outline Complete

**The vampire origin story.** Acheron the Apostate, exiled Seer of Auroria,
descends to Eldoria and creates the first vampires. He builds a hidden empire,
discovers his creation is a cosmic trap, and ultimately discards the Forbidden
Tome — setting the stage for Malakar's rise in *Vows of Silence*.

## The book

- **Title:** *Crimson Covenant*
- **Format:** ~118,000 words; prologue + 13 chapters (Parts I–III) + 3 interludes + epilogue
- **POV:** third-person limited on Acheron (interludes: Lyra, Vorenus, human scholar)
- **Timeframe:** ~500 BGD → ~100 BGD (400 years of vampire history)
- **Theme:** hubris of transcendence; the curse of creation; empire built on stolen light

## Media model

The album and its songs are one external medium telling the story; the novel is
Acheron's story in his own world. **Characters never reference the songs or the
album.** In-world knowledge comes from visions, records, the Tome, chronicles,
and ancient lore.

## Canon guardrails

1. Acheron is not a hero — he is a tyrant, a visionary, and a prisoner of his own creation.
2. The Thirst was designed by Nyktoros as a cosmic soul-harvest trap.
3. The vampires are victims, not villains.
4. Malakar is mentioned, never seen (protagonist of *Vows of Silence*).
5. The Forbidden Tome is the central object — it must be hidden at the end.
6. The media-model rule applies: no song/album references in-world.
