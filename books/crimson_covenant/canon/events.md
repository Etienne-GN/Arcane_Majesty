# Canon: Events

Purpose: Key events established by the *Crimson Covenant* novel.

## The Exile (~500 BGD)

Acheron, High Seer of Auroria, discovers Nyktoros's energy blueprints — forbidden knowledge that could grant mortals power beyond imagination. The High Council brands him a heretic and casts him out. He falls through the planes toward Eldoria, clutching the Forbidden Tome.

**Significance:** The inciting incident. Acheron chose this. He was not tricked. He believed he could control the Void.

## The Primal Pact (~450 BGD)

Acheron arrives in Eldoria and seeks out human noble bloodlines with ancient Aurorian ancestry. He approaches them with promises of transcendence. In a subterranean cavern, he proposes the Primal Pact: give up your mortality, gain eternity. Four nobles accept. They become the first vampires.

**Significance:** The creation of the vampire bloodline. The first step toward the curse.

## The Blood Ritual (~400 BGD)

Acheron executes the primordial transformation using Nyktoros's blueprints. He fuses Aurorian light with void frequencies. The four nobles are transformed — they gain strength, speed, immortality. But they also gain the Thirst. Acheron realizes too late: the Thirst was not a side effect. It was the design.

**Significance:** The creation of the Thirst. The moment Acheron realizes his mistake.

## The Phantom Realm (~350 BGD)

Acheron discovers the interstitial planar rift between Eldoria and the Void — the Phantom Realm. He learns to harvest soul energy and bank it in subterranean vaults. This is the Vampiric Soul-Leak. But the soul-banking resonates with Nyktoros. The dormant entity stirs.

**Significance:** The discovery of soul-banking. The first warning of Nyktoros's awakening.

## Lyra's Betrayal (~350 BGD)

Lyra, Acheron's first creation, realizes the Tome will destroy them all. She attempts to destroy it, fails, and is destroyed by Acheron. Her death is the first internal casualty of the Covenant.

**Significance:** The first betrayal. The vampires are not evil — they are victims.

## War of the Ancients (~300 BGD)

Human kings discover the vampires and attempt a purge. Acheron leads the Covenant in a covert war. The vampires are fewer, but stronger. They defeat the kings and force human monarchies into secret subjugation. The shadow-tax begins.

**Significance:** The rise of the vampire empire. The beginning of the shadow-war.

## Age of Shadows (~250 BGD)

The Covenant's golden era. From grand estates, vampires establish blood tithes over human cities. They live as secret aristocrats, ruling from the shadows. Acheron sits at the center, watching, controlling.

**Significance:** The height of vampire power. The golden age before the fall.

## Construction of Sanguine Vaults (~220 BGD)

Acheron commands the building of the Sanguine Vaults — a subterranean metropolis beneath Eldoria's capital. This is the Covenant's central headquarters and energy bank. He envisions a civilization that will last forever.

**Significance:** The physical manifestation of Acheron's ambition. The empire made stone.

## Rising Tide (~200 BGD)

The soul-banking accelerates. Acheron discovers the stored soul energy is resonating with the dormant Nyktoros. The Thirst was designed as a cosmic soul-harvest trap. Every vampire who feeds is feeding Nyktoros. Acheron's creation is a weapon aimed at the world.

**Significance:** The revelation. Acheron's horror at his own creation.

## Battle for Soul (~180 BGD)

Internal schism. Fanatical vampires want to surrender soul-banks to awaken Nyktoros as a god. Acheron fights to keep the energy locked. The battle is both physical and spiritual. Acheron wins, but the Covenant is fractured.

**Significance:** The end of unity. The beginning of the fall.

## Fall of the Covenant (~160 BGD)

Acheron orders the subterranean capital abandoned. The Covenant disperses into isolated cells across Eldoria. The empire is dismantled. Acheron watches his creation scatter.

**Significance:** The end of the empire. The beginning of hiding.

## The Scattered Remnants (~140 BGD)

The vampires adapt to living as clandestine secret societies. Acheron moves between hidden cells. Rumors reach him that a young Nythorian scholar named Malakar is seeking ancient dark lore.

**Significance:** The bridge to *Vows of Silence*. The first mention of Malakar.

## The Curse (~120 BGD)

Acheron reflects on the permanence of the Thirst. He realizes he permanently cursed his lineage to be eternal parasites. The curse cannot be undone. He decides the Tome must be hidden.

**Significance:** The acceptance of failure. The decision to hide the Tome.

## Beyond Horizon (~100 BGD)

Acheron journeys to the subterranean abyss of Nythoria and discards the Forbidden Tome into ancient ruins. He believes it is lost forever. He walks away, knowing it will be found.

**Significance:** The end of the story. The beginning of *Vows of Silence*.

## Book Canon Summary

- **~450 BGD:** Primal Pact, first vampires created

- **~350 BGD:** Phantom Realm discovered, Lyra's betrayal
- **~300 BGD:** War of the Ancients, shadow-tax begins
- **~250 BGD:** Age of Shadows, golden era
- **~220 BGD:** Construction of Sanguine Vaults
- **~200 BGD:** Rising Tide, Nyktoros stirs
- **~180 BGD:** Battle for Soul, internal schism
- **~160 BGD:** Fall of the Covenant, dispersal
- **~140 BGD:** Scattered Remnants, Malakar rumors
- **~120 BGD:** The Curse, reflection
