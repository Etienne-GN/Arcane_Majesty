## Book Canon Summary

The novel establishes:
- The complete history of the Crimson Covenant (~500-100 BGD)
- The creation of the first vampires
- The discovery of the Thirst as a cosmic trap
- The construction and abandonment of Sanguine Vaults
- The internal schism and fall of the Covenant
- The hiding of the Forbidden Tome at the Black Well
- The first mention of Malakar
- The media-model rule (no song/album references in-world)
