# Canon: Locations

Purpose: Key locations established by the *Crimson Covenant* novel.

## Auroria (Plane)

The celestial realm where Acheron was a High Seer. The spires of Auroria are where he studied forbidden cosmic frequencies and discovered Nyktoros. He was cast out from here ~500 BGD.

**Description:** High spires, celestial light, ancient knowledge. The seat of the old Seers. Acheron's home before his exile.

**Significance:** The origin point. The place Acheron lost.

## Eldoria (Plane)

The mortal realm where Acheron built the Crimson Covenant. Young, wild, untamed. Humanity is rising but vulnerable. Supernatural forces lurk in the shadows.

**Description:** Vast continent with diverse regions. The Emerald Fields, the Heartlands, the Scarlands, the Silver Coast, Whisper-Wood, Thaloria. The mortal realm where the vampire empire rose and fell.

**Significance:** The setting for the entire story.

## The Subterranean Cavern (~450 BGD)

Where Acheron proposed the Primal Pact to the four nobles. A hidden cavern beneath the Emerald Fields, chosen for its isolation and ancient Aurorian resonance.

**Description:** Granite halls, ancient stone, faint Aurorian glow. The place where the first vampires were created.

**Significance:** The birthplace of the vampire bloodline.

## The Blood Ritual Site (~400 BGD)

Where Acheron executed the primordial transformation. A deeper chamber within the subterranean cavern, warded against intrusion.

**Description:** Stone altar, ancient runes, void energy channels. The place where the Thirst was born.

**Significance:** The moment of transformation. The creation of the curse.

## The Phantom Realm

The interstitial planar rift between Eldoria and the Void. Acheron discovered it ~350 BGD and learned to harvest soul energy from it.

**Description:** A shadow-world between planes. Void energy swirls in currents. Soul energy can be siphoned from the fabric of reality.

**Significance:** The source of the Vampiric Soul-Leak. The connection to Nyktoros.

## Sanguine Vaults (~220 BGD)

A subterranean metropolis beneath Eldoria's capital. The Covenant's central headquarters and energy bank. Acheron commanded its construction.

**Description:** Basalt stone, carved chambers, soul-bank vaults. A city for vampires alone. The physical manifestation of Acheron's ambition.

**Significance:** The empire made stone. The heart of the Covenant.

## The Heart-Citadel

The central fortress within the Sanguine Vaults. Acheron's seat of power. Where he ruled the vampire empire.

**Description:** Grand halls, ancient architecture, soul-energy conduits. The throne room of the First Vampire.

**Significance:** The center of power. The place Acheron abandoned.

## The Battlefields (~300 BGD)

Where the War of the Ancients was fought. Human kings versus vampires. The shadow-war that established the shadow-tax.

**Description:** Scattered across Eldoria. Urban centers, rural strongholds, contested territories.

**Significance:** The rise of the vampire empire through warfare.

## The Hidden Cells (~160 BGD)

Where the vampires scattered after the fall of the Covenant. Isolated safe houses, hidden estates, secret tunnels across Eldoria.

**Description:** Various locations — hidden cabins, underground chambers, disguised estates. Each cell operates independently.

**Significance:** The beginning of hiding. The end of the empire.

## The Black Well (Nythoria, ~100 BGD)

An ancient ruin in Nythoria where Acheron discarded the Forbidden Tome. A subterranean abyss where light does not reach.

**Description:** Ancient stone, deep darkness, void resonance. The resting place of the Forbidden Tome.

**Significance:** The end of the story. The beginning of *Vows of Silence*. Where Malakar will find the Tome.

## Book Canon Summary

- **Auroria** — Acheron's home, the spires where he studied forbidden knowledge
- **Eldoria** — The mortal realm where the Covenant rose and fell
- **Subterranean Cavern** — Birthplace of the first vampires
- **Blood Ritual Site** — Where the Thirst was created
- **Phantom Realm** — The interstitial rift, source of soul-energy
- **Sanguine Vaults** — The subterranean metropolis, heart of the Covenant
- **Heart-Citadel** — Acheron's seat of power
- **Battlefields** — Where the shadow-war was fought
- **Hidden Cells** — Where vampires scattered after the fall
- **Black Well** — Where the Tome was hidden, bridge to *Vows of Silence*
