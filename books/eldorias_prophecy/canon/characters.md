# Canon: Characters

Purpose: New canon established by the *Eldoria's Prophecy* novel. Filled in per part as tasks complete.

## Oren
Cover identity: a chronicler "collecting lost libraries," traveling east. Warm, charming, genuinely knowledgeable about lore and ancient kings; superb woodcraft, selfless seeming (carries Eldrin's satchel through a squall). Meets Eldrin on the road in late winter 501 AGD; travels with him. Unstated until ch12: he is an agent of Voraun, tasked to learn the Stone's location. Asks exactly one question too many about Eldrin's destination. (Task 2)

**Full arc (Task 4):** His scholarship and kindness were real — the mask was made of true things, the hardest kind to remove. Betrays Eldrin at the Fire Gate's first landing in late spring 501 AGD: "the dagger that the shadows bring." True name unknown ("Oren" was a cover kept for years). In Voraun's service thirty years. Does not kill Eldrin — the Stone needs a hand it accepts, and Eldrin is too valuable. Voraun's touch gives him a shadow-blade arm. Takes the journal with the coordinates and descends the labyrinth ahead of Eldrin. (His later fate: the Warden has the sword.)

## The Hermit
The failed former Keeper of the Heartstone (unnamed). Once guarded the Stone; the Shadow Balrog came and he was defeated — his body broken, his spirit bound to the Sylvan Sanctuary's forest Elemental to survive; the green forest is his lingering will. Half-present, half-spirit. Grants Eldrin Aether Sight (formal mastery). Reads Oren coldly but says nothing. (Task 4)

## Malphas (Whisperer of Doubt)
One of the three Void Generals hunting the Heartstone. A *feeding* thing — feeds on the doubt of the isolated; illusions of allies and past failures; carries the winter like an instrument (the frozen camp of fourteen on the Summit of Despair is his work). Rivals the other Generals; also wants to kill their agents. Confronted by Eldrin at the Summit (ch07): Eldrin breaks his hold via Aether Sight + the refusal of fear, wounds his anchor-thread; Malphas retreats — NOT killed. Vows he'll have the Stone and will find Eldrin again where the road is loneliest. (Task 4)

## Xarathos (Pyre-Lord)
One of the three Void Generals. A fallen Aurorian corrupted by the Thirst — "was made of light and spent so long being hungry that the light has gone wrong in him." Controls the corruption of the flame; commands the Inferno Labyrinth (obsidian golems, Magma-Eaters, Cinder-Souls); the Legion seeded its defenses with Eldrin's identity. Wants the Stone to unmake the Lock and let the Void in. Cannot take the Stone himself — it rejects unworthy hands and burns them; he needs a hand the Stone accepts (Eldrin) to carry it out. Destroyed by Eldrin in the crucible (ch15) — Eldrin denies his mana-steal with a rune-drain counter, exploiting Thirst-driven arrogance. Dying words: "Voraun will have you at the door. He has been waiting the longest." (Task 4)
