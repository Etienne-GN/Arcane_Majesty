## Scholar-Magic
Runic, geometric, blue/violet, Aether-based. Costly — casting draws soul-energy ("the Snap" exhaustion stage); a lit ward acts as a "lighthouse" attracting shadow-predators and things that drink light. The masters' teaching: magic is a debt; it draws attention. (Task 2)

## Compass of the Storm
Mental compass planted by Vorgos during the vision. Always points to the Heartstone of Creation; "gives up freedom in exchange for certainty." A constant ache; trembles when near the target. (Task 2)
