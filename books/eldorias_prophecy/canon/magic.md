# Canon: Magic

Purpose: New canon established by the *Eldoria's Prophecy* novel. Filled in per part as tasks complete.

## Scholar-Magic
Runic, geometric, blue/violet, Aether-based. Costly — casting draws soul-energy ("the Snap" exhaustion stage); a lit ward acts as a "lighthouse" attracting shadow-predators and things that drink light. The masters' teaching: magic is a debt; it draws attention. (Task 2)

## Compass of the Storm
Mental compass planted by Vorgos during the vision. Always points to the Heartstone of Creation; "gives up freedom in exchange for certainty." A constant ache; trembles when near the target. (Task 2)

## Aether Sight
Gift of the Sylvan Sanctuary's Hermit (ch10). Reads "the second hand — the writing beneath the writing": hidden paths, life-threads, truth beneath masks; reveals illusions; the seer "predicts" the Balrog by reading its presence before it is seen. Not a weapon — an eye. First manifests on its own in the forest (ch09); formally mastered in ch10. (Task 4)

## Heartstone of Creation
A "Cosmic Insulator" — it stabilizes, holds, prevents; it is not a weapon and never will be, no matter how the dark hungers for it to be one. It does not shine or save; it holds the space in which saving remains possible. Rejects unworthy hands — reaching to take meets the holding as an enemy and burns; accepts its intended guardian (the whole of the road was the proving). The ward is the field of the keeping; a hand that comes to hold passes through "the way a note passes through a chord." Syncs resonance with its keeper ("the Heartbeat of Eldoria"). Used by Eldrin to burn Voraun (the ward answers any hand that reaches to take). (Task 6)

## Void-Touched Creatures
Creatures corrupted by the Void's touch or by proximity to a General's influence:

- **Shadow-Touched Wolves** (ch04): Wolves corrupted by leech-corruption; move at odd angles, green-violet eyes, drawn to warmth; vulnerable to light. The Archive's bestiary page warns they have "the shape of hunger, not the shape of a wolf."
- **Frost-Shades** (ch07): "The cold that has learned to stand." Press with cold rather than strike; move through snow like water through net; drain heat from nearby living things. Not wolves, not shadows — the mountain's anger given form.
- **Gloom-Beaks** (ch07): Black birds whose beaks drink light; wheel and scream; turn the world grey and darker. The hermit's warning: "they drink the light, and the dark is what is left."
- **Magma-Eaters** (ch14): Shapes of molten light the size of hunting hounds; rise from magma rivers; reach with hunger and drink magical light. Eldrin's wards sputter in their presence.
- **Cinder-Souls** (ch14): "The burned, the ones the fire took before their time." Shapes of ash and red light; crowd around living beings seeking lost warmth. They press close; they do not burn — they *remember* burning.
- **Obsidian Golems** (ch14): Giant obsidian figures that guard the labyrinth's paths; tuned to rune-frequencies. They step aside when the correct rune-note is played. Cannot be destroyed by force — only obeyed.

## Inner Fire
The stubborn, ordinary fire of refusal — not summoned, not fed, *answered*. When all other magic is stripped away (the Crucible burns runes, staff, will), what remains is the fire that refuses to go out. "It burns the fear... burns the doubt, and lets the inner fire out." Distinct from scholar-magic; this is the fire of the self that the Void cannot steal because it is not a resource — it is a choice. (ch14)

## Book Canon Summary
Complete set of magic facts the novel establishes (handoff payload):

- **Scholar-Magic** — runic, geometric, blue/violet, Aether-based; costly ("the Snap" exhaustion); a lit ward is a "lighthouse" drawing shadow-predators; magic is a debt.
- **Compass of the Storm** — mental compass planted by Vorgos in the vision; always points to the Heartstone; never taken by Oren (he got only the journal); sings when near the target (ch16); quiet/still at arrival (ch17).
- **Aether Sight** — the Hermit's gift; reads "the second hand — the writing beneath the writing"; hidden paths, life-threads, truth beneath masks, reveals illusions; not a weapon, an eye; mastered at the Sanctuary (ch10), first manifesting in the forest (ch09).
- **Runic reflection** — Voraun's Legion counter: mirror-marks that return a figure to its maker with its own charge (ch19).
- **The Heartstone of Creation** — "Cosmic Insulator"; holds, prevents, is not a weapon; rejects unworthy hands (burns); accepts its intended guardian; ward = the field of the keeping; syncs resonance with the keeper; burns Voraun (ch19); steadied the Lock (i4).
- **Inner Fire** — the fire of refusal; not a resource but a choice; what remains when runes, staff, will are stripped away (ch14).
- **Void-Touched Creatures** — Shadow-Touched Wolves (ch04), Frost-Shades (ch07), Gloom-Beaks (ch07), Magma-Eaters (ch14), Cinder-Souls (ch14), Obsidian Golems (ch14); corrupted by the Void's touch or a General's influence.
