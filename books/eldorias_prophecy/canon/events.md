# Canon: Events

Purpose: New canon established by the *Eldoria's Prophecy* novel. Filled in per part as tasks complete.

## The Gap
History of 0 GD was actively erased/silenced — records "tidied" rather than missing. Eldrin has spent his career proving the record is a lie. The world has catastrophically reset more than once (strata "Echoes"). (Task 1)

## The race
Legion agents are quietly asking about "the Anchor" in 501 AGD — moving in a line eastward, one question per village, never resting. Eldrin learns of them via a trader's rumor (Port Silver) at an inn. The race is real; Eldrin does not know who they are yet. (Task 2)

## Vorgos's timing (Interlude 1)
Vorgos reads the planes like a ledger. Kael's Lock is decaying faster than predicted (resonance eroding the Star-Guard from within — "eats the man to feed the door"). The three Generals' signatures converge on the ruins; Voraun's is "a subtraction," quiet for months. Vorgos moved Eldrin early because a failing Lock is a beacon. He reflects: Eldrin is not the strongest or chosen — the available piece, "a pawn upon a cosmic board." Anya (0 GD), Kael (~300 AGD), Eldrin (~500 AGD) — the line of used pieces. Almost-regret, set aside. (Task 3)

## The betrayal
Oren strikes at the first landing of the Fire Gate, late spring 501 AGD — "the dagger that the shadows bring." He is Voraun's agent (thirty years in service); seeks the Stone "to break the seal," not to guard it. The mask was made of true things — the scholarship and kindness were real, the loyalty a job. Voraun's touch arms him with a shadow-blade. Eldrin survives because Oren needs a hand the Stone accepts; Oren takes the journal (coordinates) and descends ahead. Vorgos stays silent. (Task 4)

## Malphas forced to retreat (ch07)
Eldrin breaks Malphas's illusion-hold at the Summit via Aether Sight and the refusal of fear, wounding the whisperer's anchor-thread. Malphas retreats alive — the only General of the three who survives. (Task 4)

## Xarathos destroyed (ch15)
In the crucible at the Inferno Labyrinth's heart, Eldrin denies Xarathos's mana-steal with a rune-drain counter, finding the crack in Thirst-driven arrogance. The Pyre-Lord is destroyed. His dying words: "Voraun will have you at the door. He has been waiting the longest." (Task 4)

## The Stone rejects unworthy hands
The Heartstone reads the hand that reaches for it; it will not be taken by force or deceived, and it burns the unworthy. This is the catch-22 that keeps Eldrin alive through the road: all three Generals need a hand the Stone accepts (his) to carry it out. (Task 4)
