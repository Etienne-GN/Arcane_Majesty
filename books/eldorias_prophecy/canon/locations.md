# Canon: Locations

Purpose: New canon established by the *Eldoria's Prophecy* novel. Filled in per part as tasks complete.

## Summit of Despair
"Ice-kissed peaks, a barren expanse" — the frozen high pass of the mountains east of Thaloria. Malphas's territory: he carries the winter like an instrument, and the frozen camp of fourteen Legion-less dead (plus a Crag-Fiend crowned with violet crystals) is his work. The Aether runs thin here; the site of the ch07 confrontation. (Task 4)

## Sylvan Sanctuary
"A valley green amidst the snow" — an impossible early-spring valley in the mountain country; the forest is alive, a green cathedral of arch-like trees and nameless colors. Guarded by the forest Elemental, who trials travelers on intent ("Why do you seek the light of day?") and who dismissed Oren. Home of the Hermit (failed former Keeper, spirit bound to the Elemental). Eldrin masters Aether Sight here. (Task 4)

## Fire Gate
The black-glass entrance to the Inferno Labyrinth: a stair of black glass worn by centuries, runes that hold heat and hunger and give darkness a voice, leading down into the mountain's heart. The snow-line/ash transition is the site of Oren's betrayal (ch12). (Task 4)

## Inferno Labyrinth
Xarathos's territory beneath the Fire Gate: magma rivers, a gate of flame "that calls me by my given name" (the Legion seeded its defenses with Eldrin's identity), obsidian golem puzzle-trial (rune-frequencies), Magma-Eaters and Cinder-Souls, and the heart-chamber where the Pyre-Lord waits. The heat burns away doubt — "the crucible of the mountain's core." (Task 4)
