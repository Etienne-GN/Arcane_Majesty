# Arcane Majesty: World Lore & Cosmic Structure

This document defines the fundamental cosmic and social structure of the Arcane Majesty universe.

## 1. The Tri-Planar Axis
Existence consists of three parallel dimensions separated by the **Aether**:
- **Auroria (Celestial Plane):** The high-frequency Source of divine energy. Home of the **Aurorians**.
- **Eldoria (Material Plane):** The middle-frequency realm of physical matter. Home of **Humans** and the **Crimson Covenant**.
- **Nythoria (Umbral Plane):** The low-frequency Filter. Home of the **Nythorians** and the **Legion of Souls**.

## 2. The First Apostate: Acheron's Fall
Centuries before the Great Darkness (0 GD), the Aurorian Seer **Acheron** attempted to harvest the dormant energy of the Void. He was exiled to the Material Plane for this heresy.
- **The Discovery:** In Eldoria, Acheron found ancient energy blueprints left by the dormant **Nyktoros**, intended to prepare the universe for his return.
- **The First Vampire:** By following these blueprints, Acheron harvested dormant Void energy to regain his power, transforming himself into the first being with the **Thirst**.

## 3. The Crimson Covenant: The Predator's Balance
Acheron founded the **Crimson Covenant** to manage his new race of vampires.
- **The Strategy:** Acheron realized that every human killed by a vampire sent a tormented soul to the Underworld. 
- **The Vampiric Leak:** Over centuries, this predation created a secret "leak" in the cosmic soul-cycle. Regardless of their nature, victims of vampires were dragged to the Underworld, granting Nyktoros an artificial tactical advantage over the High Plane.
- **Sustainable Predation:** To prevent Nyktoros from becoming too powerful too fast, the Covenant became a secret mafia, keeping humanity unaware and "banking" souls for the future.

## 4. The Great Darkness (0 GD)
The Great Darkness was a **Threshold Event** caused by the shift in planar balance.
- **The Awakening:** Nyktoros awakened because the shadow-energy in the Underworld finally reached critical mass, fueled by the "banked" souls of vampire victims. He achieved enough power to manifest and launch the **Legion of Souls**.
- **The Sentinel's Response:** **Aetheros (The Stormbringer)**, the sentinel of the Aether, sensed the cosmic tilt. He created the **Great Rift** and plucked **Anya** from Auroria, thrusting her into the Underworld to act as a cosmic stop-gap.
- **The Result:** Anya's sacrifice sealed the awakened Nyktoros, buying the universe time to restore the balance.

## 5. The Transference & The Harvest
- **The Insurance:** Fearing the loss of his original divine light to the Thirst, Acheron siphoned his **Aurorian Spark** and bound it to his most loyal human servant (the ancestor of **Kael**).
- **The Cosmic Harvest:** Acheron’s true goal is to use Kael (the battery of his light) to reach the sealed Nyktoros, break the lock, and siphon the Void’s awakened power into himself to achieve transcendent godhood.
