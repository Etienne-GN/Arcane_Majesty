# Chapter 3 — Odyssey's Dawn: Implementation Plan

**Goal:** replace the Chapter 3 scaffolding (a 14×10 placeholder Thaloria and an empty East Road) with a playable chapter: a real Thaloria city hub, a real East Road, its people, creatures and three quests.

**Sources (in authority order):** Neo4j (scarif) — Thaloria "the scholarly kingdom of libraries and archives", Oren (album; Voraun's agent, "a chronicler collecting lost libraries", joins on the road in late winter 501 AGD), event *The Race for the Anchor* ("Legion agents quietly ask about 'the Anchor'… Eldrin learns of them via a trader's rumor at an inn"); the book (`books/eldorias_prophecy/manuscript/part-i/ch04-odysseys-dawn.md`, `ch05-the-oblivious-road.md`, `canon/locations.md`); the campaign bible (Song 3); the map spec `data/lore/campaigns/eldorias_prophecy/thaloria_and_east_road.md`.

## Content

**Thaloria (city, ~32×26)** — generated by `tools/maps/gen_thaloria.py` from catalogued sprites (`sheetmap.py`), like the Grand Tour village:
- the college (stone, grey dome) and the bell tower with "the bell that never struck correctly" (a sign), north;
- the scholar's quarter with the door to Eldrin's Tower (existing portal), north-west;
- a cobbled square: fountain, well, signpost, market stalls, the bounty board and the hearth/alchemy stations;
- the inn (`generic_rpg_house_inn` + inn sign) with the **Innkeeper** and the **Trader** outside;
- houses from the Pipoya kit; a stone city wall with the **East Gate** (`gate_castle_arch_gray`) → East Road.
- Safe: no creatures in the city (per the map spec).

**East Road (~52×34)** — `tools/maps/gen_east_road.py`: a winter road west→east through open country, a birch copse with a campfire (the book's third-night ambush: night-only void-stalkers gather round it), a waystone with the Legion's trace, the **first Rift-Gate node**, a side spur with the optional **Corrupted Sentinel**, and the shepherd's lean-to at the mountains' foot where **Oren** waits; the far east end climbs to the Summit of Despair.
- Creatures (bestiary / spec): Void-Stalker ×4 (+3 night-only at the copse), **Blight-Ent** ×2 (new type, treant sprite), **Root-Wraith** ×3 (new type), **Corrupted Sentinel** ×1 (new type, game-only, optional miniboss, guaranteed Tier I–II drop).
- Gathering: wood ×3, ore ×2, herbs (moonpetal at night).

**Quests**
- Main **Odyssey's Dawn** (auto-starts in Thaloria; the chapter's quest): defeat void-stalkers (3) → attune the East Road monolith (the first Rift-Gate node) → speak with Oren. Reward: Iron Spell-Blade (the bible's Spell-Blade unlock), XP, insight.
- Side **Trader's Rumor** (giver: the Trader): read the waystone on the East Road where the stranger asked about "the Anchor" → return. Sets the memory fragment "The Anchor".
- Side **The Scholar's Saddlebags** (giver: the Innkeeper): your saddlebag split on the road — bring back 3 scattered journal pages (found in three spots along the road) → return.

**Engine:** signs can carry an `id`; `read_signs` steps can target one (`target: 'any'` keeps today's behaviour).

**Oren:** a dialogue choice when you meet him — "Walk, then. The road is wide enough for two." (sets `oren_joined`, the book's answer) or "I walk alone." Both complete the step.

## Not in this pass

- Oren, the Innkeeper and the Trader use the existing simple NPC sprites (tinted); a proper LPC look for Oren is a separate job (LPC Forge cast + NPC cast wiring).
- Interiors (inn, college) are not built; NPCs stand outside.
