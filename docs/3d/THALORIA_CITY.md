# Thaloria — what the city should be (design, 2026-11-20)

## What the lore says
- Lore DB: "the scholarly kingdom of libraries and archives"; the region "known for libraries, archives, and magical study".
- The book (canon/locations.md, ch01–ch04): "the scholar-city on a hill above Silverrun"; a **scholars' quarter**; the
  **college** (its chair, its tenure; the **Forbidden Archive** — iron rooms beneath the college); the **bell tower**
  that has always struck its hour "with serene inaccuracy"; the **East Gate**, where Eldrin leaves at dawn in the snow.
- The game: the Citadel on the cliff across the river gorge (the Eldoria's Prophecy cover), the Lower Town outside
  the South Gate, the North Gate's bridge and cliff stair.

## What is wrong now
The city is a 128 × 112 m square of walls round a grid of streets, with 22 houses scattered in a park — more trees than
houses, every street at a right angle, the ground flat. It reads as "houses in lines to emulate a city": a village
in a walled garden. Missing:
1. **Density** — a medieval city is houses wall to wall along every street: hundreds of buildings, not 22.
2. **An organic plan** — streets that wind, fork and climb, converging on squares; no grid, no right angle.
3. **The hill** — streets climbing toward a crown; terraces, stairs, the skyline stepping up.
4. **A real wall** — following the hill's line, with towers every 40–60 m, gatehouses with towers and portcullis.
5. **Landmarks** — above all **the college** (the city's reason to exist), then the crooked bell tower beside it,
   the temple, the market hall; a skyline you recognise from the road.
6. **The street's life** — stalls, carts, wells and fountains, shop signs, lanterns, washing lines, banners, chimney
   smoke; townsfolk in the streets; guards on the wall.
7. **Back yards and gardens** — the trees belong behind the houses, in the college's cloister and the gardens of the
   great houses, not in the streets.

## What it should look like
- **Site**: a hill on the south bank of the gorge; the **college on the crown** (highest point, seen from everywhere),
  the bell tower beside it; the **Citadel** across the gorge, higher still on its cliff.
- **Wall**: an irregular ring along the hill's contour, ~10–12 round towers; gates: **East Gate** (the road east — the
  book's), **South Gate** (the Lower Town), **West Gate** (toward the glade's road), **North Gate** (the bridge over the
  gorge, the cliff stair to the Citadel).
- **Streets**: the high street from the East Gate climbing in a long curve to the **market square** at mid-hill, on to
  the college; streets from the other gates converging on the market; lanes and alleys (2–4 m) between; stairs where
  it is steep; small squares where streets meet (a well, a fountain, a shrine).
- **Quarters**: the **Market** (the square, the market hall, stalls); the **Scholars' quarter** round the college
  (stone houses, copyists, bookbinders, the Archivists' Guild, libraries); the **Temple** with its own small square;
  **craftsmen** lower down (the smithy, the tanners toward the river); **inns** by the East Gate (travellers); the
  **great houses** (the manor) near the crown; the **barracks** by the North Gate (the bridge's guard).
- **Houses**: terraced rows sharing walls, 2–4 storeys, narrow fronts (5–8 m); stone ground floors, timber-framed
  upper floors jutting over the street; steep roofs — blue-grey slate for the scholarly city, warm tile here and
  there; chimneys, dormers, signs; deep plots with yards behind.
- **Scale**: the open world is 1:1 — a real small city is 300–500 m across. Proposed ~350 × 300 m inside the walls
  (about 300–400 buildings, merged by material so the phone carries it).

## Decided with the user (2026-11-20)
- **Size ~350 × 300 m** inside the walls (300–400 buildings).
- **Stone & slate scholar-city**: grey stone, blue-grey slate, timber-framed upper floors, many towers and spires.

- **Relief** (asked by the user): the city stands on a real hill — 8 m at the lowest gate to 36 m at the college's crown (rock under the college), the temple's knoll west of it with a hollow between, the land falling south and east; gate roads and the high street are ramps held to 16% (the ground cut and walled along them, at most ~3.5 m); lanes steeper than 14% are stairs.

## Building it (stages, each playable)
- **T1 — the plan**: the hill, the wall ring, the street network, the plots and quarters, generated (deterministic) and
  drawn as a plan image for review before anything is built.
- **T2 — the buildings**: a house generator (terraced, jettied, varied roofs and heights) merged per material, and
  the landmarks modelled: the college (library hall, cloister), the bell tower, the temple, the market hall, the
  gatehouses and towers.
- **T3 — the port**: the 16 interiors' doors set on the new buildings (inn, smithy, temple, guild hall, barracks,
  manor, houses, alchemist, market hall, bell tower), the townsfolk walking the street network, every quest and
  story position moved; the chart and minimap from the plan.
- **T4 — life**: stalls, carts, wells, lanterns lit at dusk, smoke, banners, guards on the wall, snow.

## Canon notes (Phase 10)
New names (the college's name, quarters, streets, squares) go through the canon check before they are lore. The book
puts Eldrin's tower in the scholars' quarter; the game and the DB keep it in its vale south-east of the city (settled
with the user) — the city keeps a scholars' quarter without it.
